# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US stock scanner that, after the market closes, ranks the market from overall mood down to individual stocks and explains every score it shows — it never just says "buy this."

## How it has grown

Trendora began from a deliberately empty starting line, fixing one rule that governs everything: every number is worked out once and shown the same way on every page, so a score can never disagree with itself. It became a real, openable app — a permanent left-hand menu, a dark data-focused layout, and an honest "is-the-data-fresh" badge — built on about five and a half years of real daily price history for roughly 158 stocks and funds, frozen so it runs entirely offline and answers the same way every restart.

Then the numbers reached the screen. A Sectors page ranked every sector and industry A-to-E and a Dashboard read the day's market mood from that same ranking; every stock gained three plain grades (strength, buy-point quality, risk) with a one-line reason and filters; a Themes page ranked investing themes like semiconductors and nuclear; and each stock got its own page — a candle chart with moving-average trend lines and a volume bar, its themes as clickable tags, and a plain-language line stating the price where the idea would be wrong, written by the engine, not the page.

Trendora then gained a memory: a permanent, unchangeable record of every past daily scan, browsable on a Scanner Runs page where you can reopen any earlier day — including real market-downturn days, where it correctly flagged zero stocks as worth acting on.

This latest step lets Trendora grade its own track record. A new System Health page replays past scans and measures how the stocks it graded highly actually performed afterward — by grade, by trade setup, and by market mood, against the S&P 500, the Nasdaq-100, and a fair comparison group of randomly chosen same-sector stocks, so you can tell genuine skill from a merely hot sector. You choose the time window (1 to 60 days), every figure shows how many stocks it rests on, and a prominent caveat keeps the results honest. Only a personal watchlist remains before the product is complete.

## What it can do today

The product lets users open a daily dashboard (market mood, breadth, top sectors and themes, how many stocks are actionable, the data date); browse and filter a ranked list of stocks, each with three explainable grades and a reason; open any stock's own page for its chart, its themes, and the price where the idea stops working; rank investing themes and every sector and industry; rely on every score reading the same on every page; browse a permanent history of past scans and reopen any earlier day as it stood; and open a System Health page showing, with honest sample sizes and a fair control group, whether its high grades actually predicted better returns. A personal watchlist is the last piece to come.
