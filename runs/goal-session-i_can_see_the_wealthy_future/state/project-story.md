# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US stock scanner that, after the market closes each day, ranks the market from overall mood down to individual stocks and explains every score it shows — it never just says "buy this."

## How it has grown

It began from a deliberately empty starting line: the first step built nothing, confirmed the workspace was bare, and laid out the plan — how the product would be organized for the person using it, and the one rule that governs everything: every number is worked out once and shown the same way on every page, so a score can never disagree with itself. The second step turned that plan into a real, openable app — a permanent left-hand menu, the page layout, the dark data-focused look, and an honest status badge across the top that tells you whether the data engine is connected, what the data source is, and the latest date it has. Underneath that frame we loaded the foundation everything stands on: about five and a half years of real daily price history for roughly 158 stocks and funds, stored once and frozen so the app runs completely offline and gives the same answers every restart — proven, with a real test on real history, to cover both a genuine market downturn and a genuine rally.

The third step is where the first real numbers appeared on screen. The Sectors page came alive: it now ranks every sector and industry fund from strongest to weakest, each with a simple A-to-E grade, how it's doing versus the broader market, how far it sits below its 12-month high, and a plain-English trend — and you can click any row to see exactly which ingredients produced that grade, so nothing is left as an unexplained number. The Dashboard came alive too, reading today's overall market mood as a labelled, 0-to-100 score with the reasons behind it, how broadly the market's strength is spread, the exact date the data reflects, and a short list of the top sectors that draws from the very same ranking the Sectors page shows — one source, never a second guess. And Trendora stayed honest about what it can't do yet: where a number depends on work still to come — how many stocks are actionable, or the leading themes — it plainly shows "pending" instead of a made-up zero. Next, the product turns to individual stocks and themes: scoring each name, adding stock and theme leaderboards, and filling in the dashboard's last pending tiles.

## What it can do today

The product lets users open the Sectors page and see every sector and industry fund ranked strongest-to-weakest with an explainable A-to-E grade, its strength versus the broader market, its distance below its 12-month high, and a trend label — expanding any row for the reasons behind the grade; open the Dashboard to read today's labelled, scored market mood with its drivers, how broad the market's strength is, the data date, and the top sectors; and move freely between the app's sections with an honest badge showing whether the data engine is connected. Individual-stock and theme rankings are not available yet.
