# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US stock scanner that, after the market closes, ranks the whole market — from overall mood down to individual stocks — and explains every score it shows; it never just says "buy this."

## How it has grown

Trendora began from an empty starting line with one rule that governs everything: every number is worked out once and shown the same way on every page, so a score can never disagree with itself. It became a real, openable app — a permanent left-hand menu, a dark data-focused layout, and an honest "is-the-data-fresh" badge — running entirely offline on about five and a half years of frozen daily price history for roughly 158 stocks and funds.

Then the numbers reached the screen: a Dashboard and Sectors page reading the day's market mood, a filterable list of stocks each with three plain grades (strength, buy-point quality, risk) and a one-line reason, a Themes page, and each stock's own page with a candle-and-trend chart, its themes, and the price where the idea would be wrong. Trendora gained a permanent, unchangeable memory of every past daily scan — including downturns where it correctly flagged nothing worth acting on — and a System Health page that replays history to measure how its highly graded stocks actually performed afterward, against the S&P 500, the Nasdaq-100, and a fair random same-sector peer group, always with honest sample sizes and a survivorship caveat. A personal watchlist that remembers your own note and survives a restart completed the original must-have list.

The vision then widened. Trendora learned to time-travel — a top-bar switcher shows the entire dashboard as it stood on any past trading day, served fast from saved daily snapshots rather than recomputed — and gained a Backtest page that pairs any past day's market read with a scorecard of how its top picks really performed over the next 1, 5, 10, 20, and 60 days. This most recent round taught it to spot its first chart pattern: a "volatility contraction" setup — shrinking pullbacks with volume drying up toward a breakout point. You can now filter the stock list to just these names, read a clear badge and a card explaining each one (with the price that would prove it wrong), and check on System Health whether pattern stocks actually outperformed — shown honestly even when the sample is small. Fifteen of sixteen must-haves now pass; only a plain-language glossary remains.

## What it can do today

The product lets users open a daily dashboard (market mood, breadth, top sectors and themes, how many stocks are actionable, the data date); browse and filter a ranked list of stocks, each with three explainable grades and a reason; open any stock's page for its chart, themes, and invalidation level; rank investing themes and every sector and industry; trust that every score reads the same everywhere; reopen any earlier day from a permanent scan history; time-travel the whole dashboard to any past day, served fast from saved snapshots; filter to stocks showing a volatility-contraction pattern and read why each was flagged; check a System Health page that grades — with honest sample sizes and a fair control group — whether its high grades and the new pattern predicted better returns; keep a personal watchlist that survives a restart; and open a Backtest page to read how a past day's top picks really performed.
