# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US stock scanner that, after the market closes, ranks the whole market — from overall mood down to individual stocks — and explains every score it shows; it never just says "buy this."

## How it has grown

Trendora began from an empty starting line with one rule that governs everything: every number is worked out once and shown the same way on every page, so a score can never disagree with itself. It became a real, openable app — a permanent left-hand menu, a dark data-focused layout, and an honest "is-the-data-fresh" badge — running entirely offline on about five and a half years of frozen daily prices for roughly 158 stocks and funds.

The numbers then reached the screen: a Dashboard and Sectors page reading the day's market mood, a filterable list of stocks each with three plain grades (strength, buy-point quality, risk) and a one-line reason, a Themes page, and each stock's own page with a candle-and-trend chart, its themes, and the price where the idea would be wrong. Trendora gained a permanent, unchangeable memory of every past daily scan and a System Health page that replays history to measure how its highly graded stocks actually performed afterward — against the S&P 500, the Nasdaq-100, and a fair random same-sector peer group, always with honest sample sizes and a survivorship caveat. A personal watchlist that remembers your note and survives a restart completed the original must-have list.

The vision then widened. Trendora learned to time-travel — a top-bar switcher shows the whole dashboard as it stood on any past trading day, served fast from saved snapshots — and gained a Backtest page pairing any past day's market read with a scorecard of how its top picks really performed over the next 1, 5, 10, 20, and 60 days. It then learned to spot its first chart pattern, a "volatility-contraction" setup, which you can filter for, read a clear badge and explanation for, and check on System Health. This final round delivered the last missing piece: a plain-language Methodology glossary. A new Methodology page now explains, for every grade and for the volatility-contraction pattern, what it means, the exact rules behind it, and a worked example — and a small info button on any badge in the stock list shows that same explanation in place. Because every number in the glossary is pulled live from the app's own settings, the explanations can never drift from what the scanner actually does. With that, all sixteen must-haves pass and the product is feature-complete.

## What it can do today

The product lets users open a daily dashboard (market mood, breadth, top sectors and themes, how many stocks are actionable, the data date); browse and filter a ranked list of stocks, each with three explainable grades and a reason; open any stock's page for its chart, themes, and invalidation level; rank investing themes and every sector and industry; trust that every score reads the same everywhere; reopen any earlier day from a permanent scan history; time-travel the whole dashboard to any past day; filter to stocks showing a volatility-contraction pattern and read why each was flagged; check a System Health page that grades — with honest sample sizes and a fair control group — whether its grades and the pattern predicted better returns; keep a personal watchlist that survives a restart; open a Backtest page to read how a past day's top picks really performed; and open a plain-language glossary (or tap an info button on any badge) to understand exactly what every grade and pattern means.
