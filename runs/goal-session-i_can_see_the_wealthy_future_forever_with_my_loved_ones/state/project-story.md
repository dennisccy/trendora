# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner — a decision-support and evidence-tracking platform that ranks stocks, explains every score, and proves its own usefulness through walk-forward historical evidence, without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 14 built out the full surface in waves. Dates display as YYYY-MM-DD everywhere; the dashboard gained a major-indexes chart with regime colour bands; the leaderboard gained column sorting, a live search box, and theme filtering with expandable member stocks as dated new-tab links; the Sectors leaderboard shows every ETF named and mapped to its universe; multi-date backfill runs roughly four times faster in parallel with per-stage timings; the Research Lab connects every sample-count chip to the exact stored observations; the import pipeline gained stage-aware resume, instant run history, per-date failure isolation, a per-date availability heatmap, and a live as-of calendar popover; and the event study switched to an overlap-honest first-trigger episodes default with a one-click toggle to pooled view.

Iterations 15 through 21 added further polish and closed that phase. Multi-month backfill jobs complete reliably; data removal became a deliberate, range-scoped action with a confirmation dialog; the availability heatmap gained a multi-hue colour scale with a legend; the stock-detail price chart gained a crosshair hover box; opening a historical date link no longer flashes today's figures before switching; the dashboard's market-indexes chart was changed to open on the full available history by default; and every stock now shows realized forward returns at five horizons on the leaderboard, stock detail, and Themes and Sectors pages, alongside a Regime x Setup x Pattern ranked study with NA-last sorting, filter dropdowns, and correct drill-down counts.

Iterations 22 through 24 delivered the remaining research upgrades: the Stocks leaderboard header gained a live regime label and a ranked top-five themes strip with chip badges; the as-of panel gained always-visible back/forward stepping buttons, opt-in keyboard arrow keys, and year/month jump dropdowns; Themes and Sectors leaderboards gained five forward-return columns matching Backtest exactly; and the RSP study gained NA-last sort, Regime/Setup/Pattern filter dropdowns, and a Pooled default. Two automated safety checks were reconciled after the forward-return columns were added, bringing the full 846-test suite back to completely clean.

Iteration 25 addressed a subtle rendering quirk: when someone opens a shared historical-date link, the correct date's data now appears from the very first server render — no brief flicker to today's figures and no browser console error.

Iteration 26 fixed the Expand-universe job's market-cap authentication. Previously, every attempt to screen roughly 500 candidate stocks by market cap failed silently — the app was sending an unauthenticated request that the data provider rejected with a 401, and then quietly recording zero results as if the universe were genuinely empty. Now the job acquires a standard no-key cookie and crumb from the provider once per session and reuses them across the whole batch, so real market caps come back. If the provider is temporarily unavailable, the job pauses with a clear "Resume to continue" badge and message instead of reporting an empty universe. A corrupted placeholder file left by the old broken behaviour was also cleaned up, restoring the committed seed-data protection for the 159 tracked symbols.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see today's market regime and a ranked top-five themes strip on the Stocks leaderboard header; step back to any past snapshot date using always-visible back/forward buttons, optional keyboard arrow keys, or the calendar popover with year/month jump menus; open any historical date link and see the correct data from the very first moment — no flash of today's figures; open any stock for an explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded price chart, a per-bar hover box, and realized forward returns at five horizons; sort and search the stock leaderboard by any column; filter by theme and expand member stocks as dated new-tab links; browse the Sectors leaderboard with every ETF named and mapped; see five forward-return columns on the Themes and Sectors leaderboards, colour-graded and sortable, matching Backtest exactly; run walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups; explore factor effectiveness, an event study, and a Regime x Setup x Pattern ranked study with filter dropdowns and correct NA-last sorting; click any sample count to open the exact stored observations; save stocks to a watchlist; and manage imports with live progress, stage-aware resume, per-date failure isolation, a multi-hue availability heatmap, reliable multi-month backfill, a deliberate range-scoped data-removal flow, and an Expand-universe job that now properly authenticates with the data provider and pauses with a clear Resume affordance on systemic auth failure.
