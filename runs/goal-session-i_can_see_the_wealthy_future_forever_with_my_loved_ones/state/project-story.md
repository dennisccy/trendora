# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner — a decision-support and evidence-tracking platform that ranks stocks, explains every score, and proves its own usefulness through walk-forward historical evidence, without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 14 built out the full surface in waves. Dates display as YYYY-MM-DD everywhere; the dashboard gained a major-indexes chart with regime colour bands; the leaderboard gained column sorting, a live search box, and theme filtering with expandable member stocks as dated new-tab links; the Sectors leaderboard shows every ETF named and mapped to its universe; multi-date backfill runs roughly four times faster in parallel with per-stage timings; the Research Lab connects every sample-count chip to the exact stored observations; the import pipeline gained stage-aware resume, instant run history, per-date failure isolation, a per-date availability heatmap, and a live as-of calendar popover; and the event study switched to an overlap-honest first-trigger episodes default with a one-click toggle to pooled view. The goal was declared achieved after iter-14.

Iterations 15 through 19 extended the product in-place. Multi-month backfill jobs that previously crashed mid-run now complete reliably; data removal became a deliberate, range-scoped action with a confirmation dialog; the availability heatmap gained a multi-hue colour scale with a legend; the stock-detail price chart gained a crosshair hover box showing exact date, OHLCV, percentage change, and moving-average values; and opening a historical date link no longer briefly flashes today's figures before switching — the correct data appears from the very first paint. The Dashboard's market-indexes chart was also changed to open on the full available history back to 2021 by default.

Iterations 20 and 21 delivered the final batch of research upgrades and then formally confirmed them. Iteration 20 built three substantial additions: every stock on the leaderboard now shows how it performed 1, 5, 10, 20, and 60 trading days after any historical scan date (colour-graded, honestly blank when not enough time has passed), with the same five numbers on each stock's own page; a brand-new Regime × Setup × Pattern study on the Research page shows which combinations of market regime, setup status, and detected chart pattern have historically produced the strongest risk-adjusted results as a sortable ranked table with drill-down links; and the Research page's sections load independently so a slow computation no longer freezes the page. Iteration 21 was a final housekeeping step — two small internal test-registry fixes — that turned the full 834-test automated suite completely green, formally confirming all three features and bringing the total to 75 of 78 Must-have journeys passing. The remaining three are honestly blocked by an external data provider wall and require no code change to resolve once access is available.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see today's market regime and top-ranked stocks on a dashboard whose indexes chart opens on the full available history; open any stock for an explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded price chart, a per-bar hover box, and a panel showing realized forward returns at five horizons; step back to any past snapshot date using a calendar or arrow keys with no flicker; sort and search the stock leaderboard by any column including the five forward-return horizons; filter by theme and expand member stocks as dated new-tab links; browse the Sectors leaderboard with every ETF named and mapped; run walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups; explore factor effectiveness, event studies, and a Regime × Setup × Pattern ranked study; click any sample count to open the exact stored observations; save stocks to a watchlist; and manage imports with live progress, stage-aware resume, per-date failure isolation, a multi-hue availability heatmap, reliable multi-month backfill, and a deliberate range-scoped data-removal flow.
