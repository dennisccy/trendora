# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner that helps you understand the market clearly — ranked stocks, explainable scores, walk-forward evidence, and a full research lab — without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 11 progressively built out the surface: dates display as YYYY-MM-DD everywhere, historical links survive a reload or fresh tab, the dashboard shows a major-indexes chart with regime colour bands, the leaderboard gained column sorting and a live search box, each theme's member stocks are expandable as dated new-tab links, the Sectors leaderboard shows every ETF named and mapped to its universe, multi-date backfill runs roughly four times faster in parallel with per-stage timings, and the Research Lab evidence chain connects every sample-count chip to the exact stored observations. Iterations 12 through 14 made the import pipeline materially more reliable (stage-aware resume, instant run history, live per-symbol activity lines, per-date failure isolation), added the as-of date calendar popover with a per-date availability heatmap, and made the event study overlap-honest with a first-trigger-episodes default and a one-click toggle to pooled view.

Iteration 15 closed two long-standing reliability gaps: multi-month backfill jobs that previously crashed with an internal database error now run all the way to completion, and data removal became a deliberate, range-scoped action with a confirmation dialog. Iteration 16 polished the heatmap for readability (day numbers now stand out in every cell, months ordered newest-first, two months per row so five years of coverage is compact) and added keyboard stepping for the as-of date — ArrowLeft/ArrowRight jumps between available snapshot dates while the calendar stays open. Those two additions completed the J-68–J-71 scope and the evaluator declared the goal achieved.

Iteration 17 began the next batch of improvements (J-72–J-78) with the two lowest-risk, pure-visual changes. The availability heatmap was given a clearly-separated multi-hue colour scale — empty days appear in dark slate, progressing through blue, cyan, teal-green, green, and amber for fully-covered days — along with a colour legend and improved day-number contrast in every cell. The stock-detail price chart gained a crosshair-tracking hover detail box that shows the exact date, open, high, low, close, volume, percentage change, and each moving-average value for any bar, with a clear label when the bar falls after the selected as-of date. Both changes are coded, type-checked, and source-verified correct; a browser environment issue prevented the final live screenshot check, so they remain in a "built but pending confirmation" state. The next step is a re-verification pass as soon as Chrome is available.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see today's market regime and top-ranked stocks on a dashboard with a full-history indexes chart; open any stock for a full explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded price chart; use a calendar to step back to any past snapshot date — or press ArrowLeft/ArrowRight to scrub dates with the keyboard while the calendar stays open; share or open any historical link in a new tab; sort and search the stock leaderboard by any column; filter by theme and expand each theme's member stocks as dated new-tab links; browse the Sectors leaderboard with every ETF named and mapped to its universe; run walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups and return attribution; explore factor effectiveness and overlap-honest event studies; click any sample count to open the exact stored observations; save stocks to a watchlist; and manage price-data imports with live progress, instant run history, stage-aware resume, per-date failure isolation, a compact readable availability heatmap, reliable multi-month backfill, and a deliberate range-scoped data-removal flow.
