# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner that helps you understand the market clearly — ranked stocks, explainable scores, walk-forward evidence, and a full research lab — without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 4 closed remaining surface gaps: dates display as YYYY-MM-DD everywhere, historical date links survive a reload or fresh tab, a major-indexes and regime card shows five benchmarks with color bands, and a searchable glossary of over 100 plain-language definitions lives on the Methodology page.

Iterations 5 through 11 added column sorting and new-tab links on the leaderboard, upgraded the dashboard chart to show the full stored price history with a labelled date marker, completed the Research Lab evidence chain so every sample-count chip links to the exact stored observations, made multi-date backfill run roughly four times faster in parallel with per-stage timings, added a live search box and Themes column with expandable member lists, and made the Sectors leaderboard fully legible with every ETF named, described, and mapped to its universe stocks.

Iteration 12 made the import pipeline significantly more reliable: stage-aware resume that skips already-downloaded data, instant Run History entries, live per-symbol activity lines, and per-date failure isolation so a single bad date never aborts a multi-date backfill. Iteration 13 added a per-date availability heatmap on the Data Manager page and a calendar popover for the as-of date selector. Iteration 14 made the event study overlap-honest — the same stock recurring across many consecutive scan dates is counted once at first trigger, with a one-click toggle to switch between Episodes and Pooled views. That completed the original goal.

Iteration 15 extended the goal further. Two long-standing reliability gaps in the Data Manager were closed. First, a multi-month or full-history backfill job that previously crashed partway through with an internal database error now runs all the way to completion; if a single day genuinely fails, that day is reported and isolated while every other day finishes. Second, removing imported data is now a deliberate, range-scoped action: the free-text symbols field is gone, both a From and To date are required, and the confirmation dialog shows a compact count summary — bars removed, symbols affected, snapshots that cascade away — with the Confirm button always on screen. Two remaining Must-have journeys (a more readable availability heatmap and keyboard arrow-key stepping in the as-of calendar) are deferred to iteration 16.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see today's market regime and top-ranked stocks on a dashboard with a full-history indexes chart across five major benchmarks; open any stock for a full explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded price chart; step back to any past date using a calendar that shows exactly which dates have saved snapshots; share or open any link in a new tab to land on that same dated view; sort the leaderboard by any column; search by ticker or company name; filter by theme and view each stock's theme memberships with expandable dated new-tab links; browse the Sectors leaderboard with every ETF named, described, and expandable to its universe stocks; run walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups and return attribution; explore factor effectiveness, multi-factor combinations, and setup/pattern event studies — switching between overlap-honest Episodes and Pooled views with one click; click any sample count to open the exact stored observations sortable and filterable in a new tab; save stocks to a persistent watchlist; and manage price-data imports with live per-symbol progress, instant Run History entries, stage-aware resume, per-date failure isolation, a trading-day availability heatmap, multi-month backfill jobs that now complete rather than crashing, and a deliberate range-scoped removal flow with a compact confirm dialog.
