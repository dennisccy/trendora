# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner that helps you understand the market clearly — ranked stocks, explainable scores, walk-forward evidence, and a full research lab — without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 14 built out the full surface in waves. Dates display as YYYY-MM-DD everywhere; historical links survive a reload or fresh tab; the dashboard gained a major-indexes chart with regime colour bands; the leaderboard gained column sorting, a live search box, and theme filtering with expandable member stocks as dated new-tab links; the Sectors leaderboard shows every ETF named and mapped to its universe; multi-date backfill runs roughly four times faster in parallel with per-stage timings; the Research Lab connects every sample-count chip to the exact stored observations; the import pipeline gained stage-aware resume, instant run history, per-date failure isolation, a per-date availability heatmap, and a live as-of calendar popover; and the event study switched to an overlap-honest first-trigger episodes default with a one-click toggle to pooled view. The J-55–J-67 extension brought samples sort/filter, N= new-tab chips, a glossary catalog searchable on its own page with tooltips across dense surfaces, and the jobs pipeline overhaul. After iter-14 the goal was declared achieved.

Iterations 15 and 16 extended the product in-place. Multi-month backfill jobs that previously crashed mid-run now complete reliably; data removal became a deliberate, range-scoped action with a confirmation dialog. The availability heatmap became easier to read — day numbers stand out in every cell, months are ordered newest-first, two months appear per row so five years of coverage is compact — and the as-of calendar gained keyboard stepping so ArrowLeft/ArrowRight jumps between snapshot dates while the popover stays open. The evaluator declared the goal achieved again after iter-16.

Iteration 17 shipped the two lowest-risk items from the next batch (J-72–J-78): the heatmap received a clearly-separated multi-hue colour scale progressing from dark slate through blue, cyan, teal-green, green, and amber for fully-covered days, with a colour legend; and the stock-detail price chart gained a crosshair-tracking hover box showing exact date, open, high, low, close, volume, percentage change, and each moving-average value for any bar, with a clear "after as-of (display only)" label on forward bars. Both changes were source-verified and type-checked clean, but a browser environment outage prevented live screenshot confirmation, so both remained in "built but pending" state.

Iteration 18 closed that gap. The browser environment was restored, live tests ran 9/9 PASS, and both features are now fully confirmed. The hover box evidence includes two byte-distinct screenshots showing an in-range bar and a forward bar with the "after as-of" badge. The heatmap's multi-hue scale was confirmed via live computed-CSS extraction matching the committed design tokens exactly. The critical single-date invariant — that no feature introduces a second date selector — was re-confirmed live. Five further features from the J-72–J-78 batch remain to be built.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see today's market regime and top-ranked stocks on a dashboard with a full-history indexes chart; open any stock for a full explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded price chart and a crosshair hover box showing exact OHLCV and moving-average values per bar; use a calendar to step back to any past snapshot date or press arrow keys to scrub dates; share or open any historical link in a new tab; sort and search the stock leaderboard by any column; filter by theme and expand each theme's member stocks as dated new-tab links; browse the Sectors leaderboard with every ETF named and mapped to its universe; run walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups and return attribution; explore factor effectiveness and overlap-honest event studies; click any sample count to open the exact stored observations; save stocks to a watchlist; and manage price-data imports with live progress, instant run history, stage-aware resume, per-date failure isolation, a compact multi-hue availability heatmap, reliable multi-month backfill, and a deliberate range-scoped data-removal flow.
