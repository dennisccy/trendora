# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner that helps you understand the market clearly — ranked stocks, explainable scores, walk-forward evidence, and a full research lab — without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 4 closed remaining surface gaps: dates display as YYYY-MM-DD everywhere, historical date links survive a reload or fresh tab, a major-indexes and regime card shows five benchmarks with color bands, and a searchable glossary of over 100 plain-language definitions lives on the Methodology page.

Iterations 5 through 11 added column sorting and new-tab links on the leaderboard, upgraded the dashboard chart to show the full stored price history with a labelled date marker, completed the Research Lab evidence chain so every sample-count chip links to the exact stored observations, made multi-date backfill run roughly four times faster in parallel with per-stage timings, added a live search box and Themes column with expandable member lists, and made the Sectors leaderboard fully legible with every ETF named, described, and mapped to its universe stocks.

Iteration 12 made the import pipeline significantly more reliable: stage-aware resume that skips already-downloaded data, instant Run History entries, live per-symbol activity lines, and per-date failure isolation so a single bad date never aborts a multi-date backfill. Iteration 13 added a per-date availability heatmap on the Data Manager page and a calendar popover for the as-of date selector. Iteration 14 made the event study overlap-honest — the same stock recurring across many consecutive scan dates is counted once at first trigger, with a one-click toggle to switch between Episodes and Pooled views.

Iteration 15 closed two long-standing reliability gaps. Multi-month or full-history backfill jobs that previously crashed with an internal database error now run all the way to completion, with any genuinely failing day isolated and reported while every other day finishes. Removing imported data became a deliberate, range-scoped action: both a From and To date are required, and a confirmation dialog shows a compact count summary before anything is deleted.

Iteration 16 brought the session to its final destination. The availability heatmap was polished to be immediately readable: day numbers now stand out against every cell shade using design tokens, months are ordered newest first, and two months sit side by side per row so five years of trading-day coverage is compact and scannable. The as-of calendar popover gained keyboard stepping — pressing ArrowLeft or ArrowRight jumps to the previous or next available snapshot date, with the whole app re-reading live data at the new date and the popover staying open for continued scrubbing. These two additions completed the full appended scope, and the evaluator declared the goal achieved.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see today's market regime and top-ranked stocks on a dashboard with a full-history indexes chart; open any stock for a full explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded price chart; use a calendar to step back to any past snapshot date — or press ArrowLeft/ArrowRight to scrub dates with the keyboard while the calendar stays open; share or open any historical link in a new tab; sort and search the stock leaderboard by any column; filter by theme and expand each theme's member stocks as dated new-tab links; browse the Sectors leaderboard with every ETF named and mapped to its universe; run walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups and return attribution; explore factor effectiveness and overlap-honest event studies; click any sample count to open the exact stored observations; save stocks to a watchlist; and manage price-data imports with live progress, instant run history, stage-aware resume, per-date failure isolation, a compact readable availability heatmap, reliable multi-month backfill, and a deliberate range-scoped data-removal flow.
