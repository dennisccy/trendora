# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner that helps you understand the market clearly — ranked stocks, explainable scores, walk-forward evidence, and a full research lab — without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities from a prior build cycle: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 4 closed the remaining surface gaps and achieved the original goal. Every date in the app displays as YYYY-MM-DD. Historical date links survive a reload or a fresh tab. A major-indexes and regime card on the dashboard shows the S&P 500, Nasdaq, Russell 2000, Equal-Weight S&P 500, and Dow 30 with color bands marking each market period. A searchable glossary of over 100 plain-language definitions lives on the Methodology page, and every dense analysis surface carries inline info-tooltips from the same catalog.

With the original goal met, the team approved an extension. Iterations 5 through 8 added column sorting and new-tab links on the leaderboard, upgraded the dashboard's major-indexes chart to show the full stored price history with a clearly labelled date marker, completed the Research Lab evidence chain so every sample count chip links to a drill-down of the exact stored observations, made multi-date backfill run roughly four times faster in parallel, and showed per-stage timings on every completed job card. Iterations 9 and 10 brought new ergonomics to the leaderboard and Research Lab: a live search box, a Themes column and filter dropdown, expandable theme member lists with dated new-tab links, and a click-sortable ticker-filterable samples drill-down that opens in a new tab. Iteration 11 made the Sectors leaderboard fully legible — every industry ETF now shows a config-defined name, description, and a list of exactly which universe stocks belong to it.

Iteration 12 made the import-job pipeline significantly more reliable: stage-aware resume that skips already-downloaded data, instant Run History entries the moment a job starts, live per-symbol activity lines and heartbeat timestamps, a symbols counter that cannot exceed its own total, and per-date failure isolation so a single bad date never aborts a multi-date backfill.

Iteration 13 added two new ways to understand and navigate your data. The Data Manager page now shows an availability heatmap — a full trading-day calendar grid where each square is colored by how many symbols have price bars on that date, with a distinct ring on days that also have a saved portfolio snapshot. Hovering any square shows the exact numbers; clicking one prefills the job form's date inputs for a quick backfill. The heatmap refreshes automatically after any job completes. At the same time, the date-switcher in the top navigation bar changed from a plain dropdown to a calendar popover: it highlights only real snapshot dates as selectable, greys out everything else, lets you page through months back to the oldest stored data, and offers a "Latest" button to return to the live view — all while driving the same single global date state as before.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see today's market regime and top-ranked stocks on a dashboard with a full-history indexes chart across five major benchmarks; open any stock for a full explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded price chart; step back to any past date using a calendar popover that shows exactly which dates have saved snapshots; share or open any link in a new tab to land on that same dated view; sort the leaderboard by any column; search by ticker or company name; filter by theme and view each stock's theme memberships; browse every theme's complete member list with dated new-tab links; view the Sectors leaderboard with every ETF named and described, expandable to show the exact universe stocks mapped to it; run walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups and return attribution; explore factor effectiveness, multi-factor combinations, and setup/pattern event studies in the Research Lab; click any sample count to open the exact stored observations in a new tab and sort or filter them; save stocks to a persistent watchlist; and manage price-data imports with live per-symbol progress, instant Run History entries, stage-aware resume, per-date failure isolation, and a trading-day availability heatmap that shows at a glance how much data exists for each date.
