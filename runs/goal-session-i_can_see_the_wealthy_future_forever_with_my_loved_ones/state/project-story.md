# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner — a decision-support and evidence-tracking platform that ranks stocks, explains every score, and proves its own usefulness through walk-forward historical evidence, without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 21 built out the full surface in waves. Dates display as YYYY-MM-DD everywhere; the dashboard gained a major-indexes chart with regime colour bands and a full-history default; the leaderboard gained column sorting, a live search box, and theme filtering; the Research Lab connects every sample-count chip to the exact stored observations; the import pipeline gained stage-aware resume, per-date failure isolation, a multi-hue availability heatmap, and a live as-of calendar popover with keyboard stepping; the event study switched to an overlap-honest first-trigger episodes default; the backend suite grew to over 800 tests with a continuous no-magic-numbers guard.

Iterations 22 through 28 delivered the J-79..J-86 polish extension. The Stocks leaderboard header gained a live regime label and a ranked top-five themes strip with chip badges. The as-of panel gained always-visible back/forward stepping buttons, opt-in keyboard arrow keys, and year/month jump dropdowns. Themes and Sectors leaderboards gained five forward-return columns matching Backtest exactly. The Expand-universe job now authenticates with Yahoo via cookie+crumb, surfacing a clear Resume affordance on rate-limit. A confirm-gated full snapshot rebuild was added to the Data page. Five max-drawdown columns appeared on every forward-return surface, read from the stored walk-forward calculation and colour-graded by magnitude using design tokens — shallow losses in a faint red tint, deep losses in full red. With these changes, every buildable Must-have through J-86 reached passing status.

Iteration 29 opened a new chapter: long/severe-downtrend detection. The dashboard now shows a Market Phase & Severity panel classifying the market into one of five discrete phases (Expansion, Pullback, Correction, Bear, Recovery), computing a 0–100 severity score from five named drivers, and displaying a deterministic bear probability derived by a forward Hamilton filter running over all stored observations up to the selected date. At October 2022 the panel correctly shows Bear, severity 92, bear probability near 1.00.

Iteration 30 built the next layer of this downtrend story: a full market-phase history timeline and a recovery-turn signal. The backend can now return a per-date step-function of phase and bear probability going back through every stored snapshot, group those dates into dated downtrend episodes (when they started, how severe, whether they are still open), and flag whether the current date is a causal recovery turn. A structurally fenced "retrospective" view — clearly labelled as full-sample analysis only — shows the smoothed probability and the peak-to-trough true bear dating, isolated from every live causal value so it can never bias a score or signal. A new Recovery-Turn Edge study on the Research page shows what forward returns have looked like at past recovery-turn dates, with per-horizon distributions, downside risk-adjusted figures, and count-coherent sample drill-downs. The code is complete and all backend paths are verified; a browser visual check was blocked by a transient environment issue and is scheduled for the next pass.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see today's market regime, a ranked top-five themes strip, and a Market Phase & Severity panel (phase label, 0–100 severity with named component breakdown, and a deterministic bear probability) on the dashboard. They can step back to any past snapshot date and every surface instantly shows the correct data. They can open any stock for an explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded price chart, per-bar hover details, and realized forward returns at five horizons each paired with a colour-graded max-drawdown figure. They can sort every leaderboard by any forward-return or max-drawdown column, filter and search by sector, theme, or pattern, and browse the Themes and Sectors leaderboards with ten return/drawdown columns matching Backtest exactly. They can explore factor effectiveness, event-study episodes, a Regime x Setup x Pattern ranked study, and clickable sample counts that open the exact stored observations. They can save stocks to a watchlist, trigger a confirm-gated full snapshot rebuild, and manage imports with live progress, stage-aware resume, a multi-hue availability heatmap, reliable multi-month backfill, and a deliberate range-scoped data-removal flow. A market-phase history timeline, dated downtrend-episode list, recovery-turn signal, and Recovery-Turn Edge research study are built and backend-verified — their visual appearance on screen is pending one more pass.
