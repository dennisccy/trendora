# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner that helps you understand the market clearly — ranked stocks, explainable scores, walk-forward evidence, and a full research lab — without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 14 built out the full surface in waves. Dates display as YYYY-MM-DD everywhere; the dashboard gained a major-indexes chart with regime colour bands; the leaderboard gained column sorting, a live search box, and theme filtering with expandable member stocks as dated new-tab links; the Sectors leaderboard shows every ETF named and mapped to its universe; multi-date backfill runs roughly four times faster in parallel with per-stage timings; the Research Lab connects every sample-count chip to the exact stored observations; the import pipeline gained stage-aware resume, instant run history, per-date failure isolation, a per-date availability heatmap, and a live as-of calendar popover; and the event study switched to an overlap-honest first-trigger episodes default with a one-click toggle to pooled view. The goal was declared achieved after iter-14.

Iterations 15 and 16 extended the product in-place. Multi-month backfill jobs that previously crashed mid-run now complete reliably; data removal became a deliberate, range-scoped action with a confirmation dialog. The availability heatmap became easier to read, and the as-of calendar gained keyboard stepping so arrow keys jump between snapshot dates.

Iterations 17 and 18 added the next batch of polish: a multi-hue colour scale with a legend on the heatmap, and a crosshair hover box on the stock-detail price chart showing exact date, OHLCV, percentage change, and moving-average values.

Iteration 19 fixed one of the most noticeable rough edges: opening a historical date link used to briefly flash today's figures before switching to the past date. That flash is now gone — the correct data appears from the very first paint. The Dashboard's market-indexes chart was also changed to open on the full available history back to 2021 by default.

Iteration 20 built three substantial research upgrades. Every stock on the leaderboard now shows how it performed 1, 5, 10, 20, and 60 trading days after any historical scan date — colour-graded and honestly blank when not enough time has passed — with the same five numbers available on each stock's individual page. A brand-new Regime × Setup × Pattern study on the Research page shows which combinations of market regime, setup status, and detected chart pattern have historically produced the strongest risk-adjusted results, as a sortable ranked table with drill-down links to every underlying observation. The Research page's sections also load independently so a slow computation no longer freezes the page. These three features are fully implemented and functionally verified; they are being held back only by two minor test-suite housekeeping issues (a table name to register and two code-style literals to rename) that will be resolved in the next step, after which the goal will be declared achieved.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see today's market regime and top-ranked stocks on a dashboard whose indexes chart opens on the full available history by default; open any stock for a full explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded price chart and a per-bar hover box; step back to any past snapshot date using a calendar or arrow keys, with historical data appearing immediately and without any flicker; share or open any historical link in a new tab; sort and search the stock leaderboard by any column; filter by theme and expand each theme's member stocks as dated new-tab links; browse the Sectors leaderboard with every ETF named and mapped to its universe; run walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups and return attribution; explore factor effectiveness and overlap-honest event studies; click any sample count to open the exact stored observations; save stocks to a watchlist; and manage price-data imports with live progress, instant run history, stage-aware resume, per-date failure isolation, a compact multi-hue availability heatmap, reliable multi-month backfill, and a deliberate range-scoped data-removal flow. (Forward-return columns, per-stock forward-return panels, and the Regime × Setup × Pattern study are built and functionally correct, pending the iter-21 test-suite green gate before being formally counted.)
