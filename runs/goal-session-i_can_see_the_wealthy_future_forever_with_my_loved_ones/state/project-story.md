# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner — a decision-support and evidence-tracking platform that ranks stocks, explains every score, and proves its own usefulness through walk-forward historical evidence, without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 24 built out the full surface in waves. Dates display as YYYY-MM-DD everywhere; the dashboard gained a major-indexes chart with regime colour bands and a full-history default; the leaderboard gained column sorting, a live search box, theme filtering, and a regime label with ranked top-themes strip; the Research Lab connects every sample-count chip to the exact stored observations; the import pipeline gained stage-aware resume, a multi-hue availability heatmap, a live as-of calendar popover with keyboard stepping, and a global as-of URL that deep-links to any snapshot date. The as-of panel gained stepping buttons and year/month jump dropdowns. Themes and Sectors leaderboards gained five forward-return columns matching Backtest exactly; the Expand-universe job authenticates with Yahoo via cookie and crumb; a confirm-gated full snapshot rebuild was added; and five max-drawdown columns appeared on every forward-return surface, colour-graded by magnitude.

Iterations 25 through 28 extended the polish layer: as-of deep links now render correctly from the first paint with no hydration mismatch, synchronous URL hydration prevents any date flash, and the backward-history extension flow became a confirm-gated control on the Data Manager.

Iterations 29 through 32 opened the market-intelligence chapter. The dashboard gained a Market Phase & Severity panel, a phase history timeline with dated causal downtrend episodes, a structurally fenced retrospective sub-view, a recovery-turn signal, and a Recovery-Turn Edge study. The Research page gained a Downtrend Opportunity study grouping past observations by causal market phase. The Data Manager gained a macro feed panel listing FRED economic series.

Iterations 33 and 34 tackled the dynamic point-in-time universe. The stock scoring engine was rewired to resolve membership per historical date — stepping back in time now honestly shows a smaller universe (empty before ~October 2021, growing toward full membership by early 2022). Three new Data Manager panels were built: a per-date breakdown of admitted versus excluded stocks with reasons, a membership timeline with entries and exits, and a confirm-gated backward-history extension control. Iteration 34 ran the live browser pass: the coverage diagnostic and backward-history control panels now render confirmed live, and the methodology page now shows the per-date membership rule prose. The stocks leaderboard itself still shows the old static count at all dates — the resolver is built and correct, but a full snapshot rebuild (~11 hours) is needed to persist the dynamic membership into the stored pages.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see a live dashboard with a regime score, Market Phase & Severity panel, phase history timeline, and dated downtrend episodes with a fenced retrospective sub-view. They can explore a Recovery-Turn Edge study and a Downtrend Opportunity study on the Research page. They can step to any past snapshot date and every surface re-points instantly. The Data Manager shows per-date admitted and excluded stock counts with reasons, a membership timeline, a macro feed panel, and confirm-gated controls for a full rebuild and backward-history extension. They can open any stock for an explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded chart, per-bar hover details, and five forward-return columns each paired with a colour-graded drawdown figure, sort every leaderboard by forward-return or drawdown, filter and search by sector, theme, or pattern, and click any sample count to see the exact stored observations. They can save stocks to a watchlist and manage imports with live progress tracking.
