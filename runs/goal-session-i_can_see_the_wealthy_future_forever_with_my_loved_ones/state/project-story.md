# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner — a decision-support and evidence-tracking platform that ranks stocks, explains every score, and proves its own usefulness through walk-forward historical evidence, without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 14 built out the full surface in waves. Dates display as YYYY-MM-DD everywhere; the dashboard gained a major-indexes chart with regime colour bands; the leaderboard gained column sorting, a live search box, and theme filtering with expandable member stocks as dated new-tab links; the Sectors leaderboard shows every ETF named and mapped to its universe; multi-date backfill runs roughly four times faster in parallel with per-stage timings; the Research Lab connects every sample-count chip to the exact stored observations; the import pipeline gained stage-aware resume, instant run history, per-date failure isolation, a per-date availability heatmap, and a live as-of calendar popover; and the event study switched to an overlap-honest first-trigger episodes default with a one-click toggle to pooled view.

Iterations 15 through 19 extended the product further. Multi-month backfill jobs complete reliably; data removal became a deliberate, range-scoped action with a confirmation dialog; the availability heatmap gained a multi-hue colour scale with a legend; the stock-detail price chart gained a crosshair hover box; opening a historical date link no longer flashes today's figures before switching; and the dashboard's market-indexes chart was changed to open on the full available history by default.

Iterations 20 through 24 delivered the remaining research upgrades and closed that phase. Every stock shows realized forward returns at five horizons on the leaderboard, stock detail, and Themes and Sectors pages; a Regime x Setup x Pattern study shows which combinations have historically produced the strongest results, with NA-last sorting, three filter dropdowns, and correct N= chip drill-downs; the top bar gained always-visible back/forward stepping buttons and an opt-in keyboard shortcut; and two automated safety checks were reconciled after forward-return columns were added, bringing the full 846-test suite back to completely clean.

Iteration 25 addressed a subtle but user-visible rendering quirk: when someone shares a link to a specific historical date and a recipient opens it in a new tab or reloads it, the page now shows the correct date's badge and navigation links from the very first moment — no brief flicker to today's data, and no browser console error. A new Next.js middleware reads the date from the URL and passes it to the server-side layout before the page is sent, so the server and the browser start from exactly the same date. Three new capabilities are queued for the next rounds: authenticating with the data provider to expand the tracked universe to roughly 500 stocks, a confirm-gated tool to rebuild historical snapshots from scratch, and max-drawdown columns on every forward-return surface.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see today's market regime and a ranked top-five themes strip on the Stocks leaderboard header; step back to any past snapshot date using always-visible back/forward buttons, optional keyboard arrow keys, or the calendar popover with year/month jump menus; open any historical date link and see the correct data from the very first moment — no flash of today's figures; open any stock for an explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded price chart, a per-bar hover box, and realized forward returns at five horizons; sort and search the stock leaderboard by any column; filter by theme and expand member stocks as dated new-tab links; browse the Sectors leaderboard with every ETF named and mapped; see five forward-return columns on the Themes and Sectors leaderboards, colour-graded and sortable, matching Backtest exactly; run walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups; explore factor effectiveness, an event study, and a Regime x Setup x Pattern ranked study with filter dropdowns and correct NA-last sorting; click any sample count to open the exact stored observations; save stocks to a watchlist; and manage imports with live progress, stage-aware resume, per-date failure isolation, a multi-hue availability heatmap, reliable multi-month backfill, and a deliberate range-scoped data-removal flow.
