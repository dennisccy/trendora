# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner — a decision-support and evidence-tracking platform that ranks stocks, explains every score, and proves its own usefulness through walk-forward historical evidence, without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 21 built out the full surface in waves. Dates display as YYYY-MM-DD everywhere; the dashboard gained a major-indexes chart with regime colour bands and a full-history default; the leaderboard gained column sorting, a live search box, and theme filtering; the Research Lab connects every sample-count chip to the exact stored observations; the import pipeline gained stage-aware resume, per-date failure isolation, a multi-hue availability heatmap, and a live as-of calendar popover with keyboard stepping; the event study switched to an overlap-honest first-trigger episodes default; the backend suite grew to over 800 tests with a continuous no-magic-numbers guard.

Iterations 22 through 28 delivered a polish extension: the Stocks leaderboard header gained a live regime label and a ranked top-five themes strip with chip badges; the as-of panel gained back/forward stepping buttons, opt-in keyboard arrow keys, and year/month jump dropdowns; Themes and Sectors leaderboards gained five forward-return columns matching Backtest exactly; the Expand-universe job now authenticates with Yahoo via cookie and crumb; a confirm-gated full snapshot rebuild was added; and five max-drawdown columns appeared on every forward-return surface, colour-graded by magnitude.

Iterations 29 through 31 opened the market-intelligence chapter. Iteration 29 added the Market Phase & Severity panel to the dashboard — one of five discrete phases, a 0–100 severity score from five named drivers, and a deterministic bear probability from a forward Hamilton filter. Iteration 30 built a full market-phase history timeline, dated causal downtrend episodes, a causal recovery-turn signal, a structurally fenced retrospective sub-view (clearly labelled as analysis-only, never feeding any score), and a Recovery-Turn Edge study on the Research page. Iteration 31 brought the environment back up and ran a full 13/13 browser-QA pass confirming everything on screen: the step-function timeline, the 2022 bear episode, the toggled retrospective with smoothed probability and peak-to-trough true-bear dating, and the Research lab with count-coherent N= drill-downs verified same-instant across all modes.

Iteration 32 extended the Research page with a Downtrend Opportunity study that groups past walk-forward observations by the causal market phase, severity band, or bear-probability band at the time — showing which stocks held up best and which fell hardest under each condition, along with per-horizon forward returns, downside risk figures, and count-coherent sample drill-downs. A "research evidence only" label is permanently attached to the fell-hardest angle. The Data Manager gained a macro feed panel listing four FRED economic series (Treasury spread, unemployment, credit spread, dollar index) with their publication lags and proxy tickers; all macro inputs remain off by default and the existing dashboard scores are byte-identical unchanged. J-93 through J-96 — a dynamic point-in-time universe resolver that determines which stocks were eligible on each historical date — are the remaining unbuilt Must-haves.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see a live market dashboard with a regime score, a Market Phase & Severity panel (phase label, 0–100 severity, five named component drivers, causal bear probability), a scrollable phase history timeline, and dated downtrend episodes. They can toggle a fenced retrospective sub-view showing full-sample smoothed probability and true-bear dating for research purposes. They can see a recovery-turn signal and explore the Recovery-Turn Edge study on the Research page. They can explore a new Downtrend Opportunity study grouping historical observations by the market phase, severity band, or bear-probability band at the time — with per-horizon forward returns, downside-risk figures, a mandatory evidence-only label on the fell-hardest angle, and count-coherent sample drill-downs. They can view a Data Manager macro feed panel showing four FRED series and their availability status. They can step to any past snapshot date and every surface re-points instantly. They can open any stock for an explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded price chart, per-bar hover details, realized forward returns at five horizons each paired with a colour-graded max-drawdown figure, sort every leaderboard by forward-return or drawdown columns, filter and search by sector, theme, or pattern, and click any sample count to see the exact stored observations. They can save stocks to a watchlist, manage imports with live progress tracking, and trigger a confirm-gated full snapshot rebuild.
