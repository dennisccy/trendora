# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner — a decision-support and evidence-tracking platform that ranks stocks, explains every score, and proves its own usefulness through walk-forward historical evidence, without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 14 built out the full surface in waves. Dates display as YYYY-MM-DD everywhere; the dashboard gained a major-indexes chart with regime colour bands; the leaderboard gained column sorting, a live search box, and theme filtering with expandable member stocks as dated new-tab links; the Sectors leaderboard shows every ETF named and mapped to its universe; multi-date backfill runs roughly four times faster in parallel with per-stage timings; the Research Lab connects every sample-count chip to the exact stored observations; the import pipeline gained stage-aware resume, instant run history, per-date failure isolation, a per-date availability heatmap, and a live as-of calendar popover; and the event study switched to an overlap-honest first-trigger episodes default with a one-click toggle to pooled view.

Iterations 15 through 21 added further polish. Multi-month backfill jobs complete reliably; data removal became a deliberate, range-scoped action with a confirmation dialog; the availability heatmap gained a multi-hue colour scale with a legend; the stock-detail price chart gained a crosshair hover box; opening a historical date link no longer flashes today's figures before switching; the dashboard's market-indexes chart was changed to open on the full available history by default; and every stock now shows realized forward returns at five horizons alongside a Regime x Setup x Pattern ranked study with NA-last sorting, filter dropdowns, and correct drill-down counts.

Iterations 22 through 24 delivered research upgrades: the Stocks leaderboard header gained a live regime label and a ranked top-five themes strip with chip badges; the as-of panel gained always-visible back/forward stepping buttons, opt-in keyboard arrow keys, and year/month jump dropdowns; Themes and Sectors leaderboards gained five forward-return columns matching Backtest exactly.

Iterations 25 through 27 closed the J-83..J-86 extension in sequence. Iteration 25 fixed a subtle server-rendering quirk so historical date links show the correct data from the very first paint, with no flicker. Iteration 26 fixed the Expand-universe job's market-cap authentication so real caps are fetched with a Yahoo cookie+crumb flow, surfacing a clear Resume affordance when the provider is temporarily rate-limited. Iteration 27 added the final layer of downside-risk context: a coverage diagnostic on the Data page, a confirm-gated full snapshot rebuild that clears and regenerates all snapshots without touching the committed price seed, and five max-drawdown columns on every forward-return surface — stocks, themes, sectors, stock detail, backtest breakdowns, and research tables — read from the same stored walk-forward calculation, never recomputed. The backend suite reached 878 tests passing with two small frontend display issues (flat MDD colour and a sort-button selector false-negative) left for one final lean pass.

Iteration 28 closed the session. A single shared helper (`lib/mdd-color.ts`) replaced the flat single-red MDD colour with a magnitude-graded scale: a shallow drawdown shows a faint red tint while a catastrophic one shows full red, all expressed through `color-mix` over the app's existing design tokens with no hardcoded colour anywhere. The "sort no-op" from iteration 27 was confirmed to be a browser-QA selector false-negative — the sort code was byte-unchanged and verified working by aria-label on all five MDD columns and all five forward-return columns. With this 3-file frontend change, every buildable capability is green.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see today's market regime and a ranked top-five themes strip on the Stocks leaderboard header; step back to any past snapshot date using always-visible back/forward buttons, optional keyboard arrow keys, or the calendar popover with year/month jump menus; open any historical date link and see the correct data from the very first moment; open any stock for an explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded price chart, a per-bar hover box, and realized forward returns at five horizons — each paired with a colour-graded max-drawdown figure where deeper red means a more severe loss; sort every leaderboard by any forward-return or max-drawdown column with NA values sorted last; filter and search the stock leaderboard by any column, theme, sector, or pattern; browse the Themes and Sectors leaderboards with five forward-return and five max-drawdown columns matching Backtest exactly; run walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups and average max-drawdown per breakdown group; explore factor effectiveness, event-study episodes, and a Regime x Setup x Pattern ranked study with filter dropdowns and correct drill-down counts; click any sample count to open the exact stored observations; save stocks to a watchlist; trigger a confirm-gated full snapshot rebuild from the Data page; and manage imports with live progress, stage-aware resume, per-date failure isolation, a multi-hue availability heatmap, reliable multi-month backfill, a deliberate range-scoped data-removal flow, and an Expand-universe job that properly authenticates with the data provider.
