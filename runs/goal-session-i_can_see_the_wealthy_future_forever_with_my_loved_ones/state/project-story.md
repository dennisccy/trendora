# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner — a decision-support and evidence-tracking platform that ranks stocks, explains every score, and proves its own usefulness through walk-forward historical evidence, without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 14 built out the full surface in waves. Dates display as YYYY-MM-DD everywhere; the dashboard gained a major-indexes chart with regime colour bands; the leaderboard gained column sorting, a live search box, and theme filtering with expandable member stocks as dated new-tab links; the Sectors leaderboard shows every ETF named and mapped to its universe; multi-date backfill runs roughly four times faster in parallel with per-stage timings; the Research Lab connects every sample-count chip to the exact stored observations; the import pipeline gained stage-aware resume, instant run history, per-date failure isolation, a per-date availability heatmap, and a live as-of calendar popover; and the event study switched to an overlap-honest first-trigger episodes default with a one-click toggle to pooled view.

Iterations 15 through 19 extended the product further. Multi-month backfill jobs complete reliably; data removal became a deliberate, range-scoped action with a confirmation dialog; the availability heatmap gained a multi-hue colour scale with a legend; the stock-detail price chart gained a crosshair hover box; opening a historical date link no longer flashes today's figures before switching; and the dashboard's market-indexes chart was changed to open on the full available history by default.

Iterations 20 and 21 delivered the final batch of research upgrades: every stock shows realized forward returns at five horizons on both the leaderboard and the stock detail page; a Regime x Setup x Pattern study on the Research page shows which combinations have historically produced the strongest results; and a test-suite consolidation confirmed everything green.

Iteration 22 added two polish improvements: the top bar gained always-visible back/forward buttons to step the viewed date one snapshot at a time, plus an opt-in keyboard shortcut and year/month calendar jump menus; the Stocks leaderboard header gained the selected date's regime label and score and a ranked strip of the top five themes.

Iteration 23 extended forward-return evidence to the Themes and Sectors leaderboards. Each theme now shows its equal-weight member-basket realized return at 1, 5, 10, 20, and 60 days — colour-graded, NA-honest, and sortable — and each sector/industry ETF shows its own stored return at the same horizons, byte-identical to the Backtest page. The Regime x Setup x Pattern research table also received four fixes: NA-last sorting, three filter dropdowns, correct N= chip drill-downs, and a Pooled-by-default toggle.

Iteration 24 was the final behind-the-scenes consolidation that closed the session. Two automated safety checks had become stale after iteration 23's forward-return columns were added, so they were reconciled to correctly recognise the new columns as a legitimate addition. The full automated test suite — 846 tests — was rerun and came back completely clean, satisfying the last remaining gate. No page, figure, or flow changed. With every planned capability built, verified, and tested, the goal was declared achieved.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see today's market regime, regime score, and a ranked top-five themes strip on the Stocks leaderboard header; step back to any past snapshot date using always-visible back/forward buttons, optional keyboard arrow keys, or the calendar popover with year/month jump menus; open any stock for an explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded price chart, a per-bar hover box, and realized forward returns at five horizons; sort and search the stock leaderboard by any column; filter by theme and expand member stocks as dated new-tab links; browse the Sectors leaderboard with every ETF named and mapped; see five forward-return columns on the Themes and Sectors leaderboards, colour-graded and sortable, matching Backtest exactly; run walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups; explore factor effectiveness, an event study, and a Regime x Setup x Pattern ranked study with filter dropdowns and correct NA-last sorting; click any sample count to open the exact stored observations; save stocks to a watchlist; and manage imports with live progress, stage-aware resume, per-date failure isolation, a multi-hue availability heatmap, reliable multi-month backfill, and a deliberate range-scoped data-removal flow.
