# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner that helps you understand the market clearly — ranked stocks, explainable scores, walk-forward evidence, and a full research lab — without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities from a prior build cycle: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 4 closed the remaining surface gaps and achieved the original goal. Every date in the app displays as YYYY-MM-DD. Historical date links survive a reload or a fresh tab. A major-indexes and regime card on the dashboard shows the S&P 500, Nasdaq, Russell 2000, Equal-Weight S&P 500, and Dow 30 with color bands marking each market period. A searchable glossary of over 100 plain-language definitions lives on the Methodology page, and every dense analysis surface carries inline info-tooltips from the same catalog.

With the original goal met, the team approved an extension. Iterations 5 through 8 added column sorting and new-tab links on the leaderboard, upgraded the dashboard's major-indexes chart to show the full stored price history with a clearly labelled date marker, completed the Research Lab evidence chain so every "N=" chip links to a drill-down of the exact stored observations, made multi-date backfill run roughly four times faster in parallel, and showed per-stage timings on every completed job card.

With that extension complete, the team approved thirteen more journeys. Iteration 9 added a live search box to the leaderboard, a Themes column showing each stock's memberships, and a Theme filter dropdown; the Themes page "+n" button now expands to show every member with dated new-tab links. Iteration 10 brought the same ergonomics to the Research Lab's evidence drill-down — the samples table is click-sortable on every column, a ticker filter box narrows the visible rows as you type, and clicking any "N=" chip opens the drill-down in a new tab so your lab selections stay undisturbed.

Iteration 11 made the Sectors leaderboard fully legible. Before this iteration, industry ETF rows showed only bare ticker codes like "KRE" or "SMH". Now every ETF row carries a real config-defined name and, when expanded, a plain-language description and a list of exactly which universe stocks belong to that sector or industry group. Up to six member tickers appear right away with a "+N" button to reveal the rest — each chip a dated new-tab link to that stock's detail page. An ETF with no mapped members shows an honest empty state. Scores, ranks, and all other numbers are completely unchanged. The next target is the jobs-pipeline cluster: stage-aware resume, live fine-grained progress, and reliable concurrent multi-date backfill.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see today's market regime and top-ranked stocks on a dashboard with a full-history indexes chart spanning five major benchmarks; open any stock for a full explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded price chart; step back to any past date with a single global switcher so every page reflects that exact stored snapshot; share or middle-click any link to land on that dated view; sort the leaderboard by any column; search the leaderboard instantly by ticker or company name; filter by theme and see each stock's theme memberships in the table; browse every theme's complete member list and jump to any member's dated detail in a new tab; view the Sectors leaderboard with every ETF named and described from config, and expand any row to see the exact universe stocks mapped to that sector or industry group; run walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups and return attribution; explore factor effectiveness, multi-factor combinations, and setup/pattern event studies in the Research Lab; click any "N=" chip to open the exact stored observations in a new tab and then sort or filter those observations by ticker or any column without changing the published count; jump to any stock's dated detail from a sample row; save stocks to a persistent watchlist; and manage price-data imports with per-stage timings on every completed job.
