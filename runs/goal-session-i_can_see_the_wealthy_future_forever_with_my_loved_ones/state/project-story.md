# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner — a decision-support and evidence-tracking platform that ranks stocks, explains every score, and proves its own usefulness through walk-forward historical evidence, without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 21 built out the full surface in waves. Dates display as YYYY-MM-DD everywhere; the dashboard gained a major-indexes chart with regime colour bands and a full-history default; the leaderboard gained column sorting, a live search box, and theme filtering; the Research Lab connects every sample-count chip to the exact stored observations; the import pipeline gained stage-aware resume, per-date failure isolation, a multi-hue availability heatmap, and a live as-of calendar popover with keyboard stepping; the event study switched to an overlap-honest first-trigger episodes default; the backend suite grew to over 800 tests with a continuous no-magic-numbers guard.

Iterations 22 through 28 delivered the J-79..J-86 polish extension. The Stocks leaderboard header gained a live regime label and a ranked top-five themes strip with chip badges. The as-of panel gained always-visible back/forward stepping buttons, opt-in keyboard arrow keys, and year/month jump dropdowns. Themes and Sectors leaderboards gained five forward-return columns matching Backtest exactly. The Expand-universe job now authenticates with Yahoo via cookie+crumb, surfacing a clear Resume affordance on rate-limit. A confirm-gated full snapshot rebuild was added to the Data page. Five max-drawdown columns appeared on every forward-return surface, read from the stored walk-forward calculation and colour-graded by magnitude using design tokens.

Iterations 29 through 31 opened and completed the market-intelligence chapter. Iteration 29 added the Market Phase & Severity panel to the dashboard: one of five discrete phases (Expansion, Pullback, Correction, Bear, Recovery), a 0–100 severity score from five named drivers, and a deterministic bear probability from a forward Hamilton filter. Iteration 30 built the next layer: a full market-phase history timeline going back through every stored snapshot, dated causal downtrend episodes, a causal recovery-turn signal, a structurally fenced retrospective sub-view (clearly labelled as full-sample analysis only, never feeding any score), and a Recovery-Turn Edge study on the Research page. The code was complete and backend-proven; a transient environment issue blocked the visual check. Iteration 31 brought the environment back up and ran a full 13/13 browser-QA pass, confirming everything on screen: the step-function timeline, the 2022 bear episode, the toggled retrospective with smoothed probability and peak-to-trough true-bear dating, the honest empty state at an early date, the recovery-turn signal with its named reason, and the Research lab with count-coherent N= drill-downs verified same-instant across all modes.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see a live market regime, a ranked top-five themes strip, and a Market Phase & Severity panel with a phase label, severity score, five named component drivers, a causal bear probability, a scrollable phase timeline, and dated downtrend episodes. They can toggle a fenced retrospective sub-view showing full-sample smoothed probability and true-bear dating for research purposes only. They can see a recovery-turn signal with an explicit reason and explore the Recovery-Turn Edge research study on the Research page showing per-horizon forward-return edge and downside risk-adjusted figures at past recovery dates. They can step to any past snapshot date and every surface re-points instantly. They can open any stock for an explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded price chart, per-bar hover details, realized forward returns at five horizons each paired with a colour-graded max-drawdown figure, sort every leaderboard by forward-return or drawdown columns, filter and search by sector, theme, or pattern, and click any sample count to see the exact stored observations. They can save stocks to a watchlist, manage imports with live progress tracking, and trigger a confirm-gated full snapshot rebuild.
