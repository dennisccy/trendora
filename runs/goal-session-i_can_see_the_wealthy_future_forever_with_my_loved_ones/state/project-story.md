# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner that helps you understand the market clearly — ranked stocks, explainable scores, walk-forward evidence, and a full research lab — without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities from a prior build cycle: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 4 closed the remaining surface gaps and achieved the original goal. Every date in the app displays as YYYY-MM-DD. Historical date links survive a reload or a fresh tab. A major-indexes and regime card on the dashboard shows the S&P 500, Nasdaq, Russell 2000, Equal-Weight S&P 500, and Dow 30 with color bands marking each market period. A searchable glossary of over 100 plain-language definitions lives on the Methodology page, and every dense analysis surface carries inline info-tooltips from the same catalog.

With the original goal met, the team approved an extension. Iterations 5 through 8 added column sorting and new-tab links on the leaderboard, upgraded the dashboard's major-indexes chart to show the full stored price history with a clearly labelled date marker, completed the Research Lab evidence chain so every sample count chip links to a drill-down of the exact stored observations, made multi-date backfill run roughly four times faster in parallel, and showed per-stage timings on every completed job card.

With that extension complete, the team approved thirteen more journeys. Iterations 9 and 10 brought new ergonomics to the leaderboard and Research Lab: a live search box, a Themes column and filter dropdown, expandable theme member lists with dated new-tab links, and a click-sortable ticker-filterable samples drill-down that opens in a new tab so lab selections stay undisturbed. Iteration 11 made the Sectors leaderboard fully legible — every industry ETF now shows a config-defined name, a plain-language description, and a list of exactly which universe stocks belong to it, with honest empty states for ETFs with no mapped members.

Iteration 12 made the import-job pipeline significantly more reliable. A job that successfully downloads data but then fails during the analysis step now remembers where it stopped; hitting "Resume" skips the download entirely and picks up at analysis, saving time and provider quota. A "running" row appears in Run History the instant a job starts — not only when it finishes. While a job is active, a live activity line reports which stock or date is being processed right now, a heartbeat timestamp confirms progress is still live, and the symbols counter can no longer count past its own total. If one date in a multi-date batch fails, that date is logged honestly and the remaining dates complete — the job does not abort and no fake snapshot is created. A deployment bug caught during testing (two database columns not registered for automatic migration) was fixed in the same iteration and guarded with new regression tests.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see today's market regime and top-ranked stocks on a dashboard with a full-history indexes chart spanning five major benchmarks; open any stock for a full explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded price chart; step back to any past date with a single global switcher so every page reflects that exact stored snapshot; share or middle-click any link to land on that dated view; sort the leaderboard by any column; search the leaderboard instantly by ticker or company name; filter by theme and see each stock's theme memberships in the table; browse every theme's complete member list and jump to any member's dated detail in a new tab; view the Sectors leaderboard with every ETF named and described from config, and expand any row to see the exact universe stocks mapped to that sector or industry group; run walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups and return attribution; explore factor effectiveness, multi-factor combinations, and setup/pattern event studies in the Research Lab; click any sample count to open the exact stored observations in a new tab and sort or filter them without disturbing the lab view; save stocks to a persistent watchlist; and manage price-data imports with live per-symbol/per-date progress, instant Run History entries, stage-aware resume that skips already-completed work, and per-date failure isolation so a single bad date never aborts the whole job.
