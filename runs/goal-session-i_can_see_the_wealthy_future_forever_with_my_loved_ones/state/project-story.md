# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner — a decision-support and evidence-tracking platform that ranks stocks, explains every score, and proves its own usefulness through walk-forward historical evidence, without placing orders or touching real money.

## How it has grown

The product arrived at this session already carrying a full, verified set of capabilities: a live dashboard with a market-regime score, a ranked stock leaderboard filterable by sector or pattern, individual stock pages with a price chart and a plain-language breakdown of every score component, walk-forward backtest evidence with control groups, a Factor Research Lab, a persistent watchlist, and a Data Manager that handles imports, coverage gaps, and interrupted jobs.

Iterations 1 through 24 built out the full surface in waves. Dates display as YYYY-MM-DD everywhere; the dashboard gained a major-indexes chart, a regime label with a ranked top-themes strip, and a global as-of URL that deep-links to any snapshot date with stepping buttons and year/month jump dropdowns. The Research Lab connects every sample-count chip to the exact stored observations. Themes and Sectors leaderboards gained five forward-return columns matching Backtest exactly; the Expand-universe job gained Yahoo cookie-and-crumb authentication; and five colour-graded max-drawdown columns appeared on every forward-return surface. As-of deep links render correctly from the first paint with no hydration mismatch.

Iterations 29 through 32 opened the market-intelligence chapter. The dashboard gained a Market Phase & Severity panel, a phase history timeline with dated causal downtrend episodes, a structurally fenced retrospective sub-view, a recovery-turn signal, and a Recovery-Turn Edge study. The Research page gained a Downtrend Opportunity study. The Data Manager gained a macro feed panel listing FRED economic series.

Iterations 33 and 34 tackled the dynamic point-in-time universe. The stock scoring engine was rewired to resolve membership per historical date, and three new Data Manager panels were built: a per-date breakdown of admitted versus excluded stocks with reasons, a membership timeline with entries and exits, and a coverage diagnostic. Live browser passes confirmed the coverage diagnostic and backward-history control panels render correctly.

Iteration 35 ran an operator-confirmed overnight rebuild of all historical snapshots. The rebuild succeeded: the stocks leaderboard now shows the honest point-in-time universe for every past date — empty before October 2021, growing naturally to roughly 544 stocks by the latest date, fixing the long-standing flat-122 problem. However, the same data growth exposed a performance gap: the Data Manager page began hanging on load because its coverage calculation had to loop through all 1,369 historical dates without a cache, making it unresponsive.

Iteration 36 fixed the hang. Rather than recomputing the membership timeline on every page load, the system now computes it once, saves the result in a dedicated cache table (keyed to the dataset version), and serves the saved result on every subsequent request — exactly the same pattern already used for the Market Phase and Research event-study caches. The background startup process also pre-warms the cache after boot, so the very first page load after a restart is already a cache hit. Response time dropped from a five-minute hang to roughly fifteen seconds, with byte-for-byte identical numbers. All code gates passed (review, QA, audit, coherence, closure); live browser confirmation that the coverage diagnostic and membership growth chart render again is the final remaining gate before the product can be declared complete.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see a live dashboard with a regime score, Market Phase & Severity panel, phase history timeline, and dated downtrend episodes with a fenced retrospective sub-view. They can explore a Recovery-Turn Edge study and a Downtrend Opportunity study on the Research page. They can step to any past snapshot date and have every surface re-point instantly. The stocks leaderboard shows the honest point-in-time universe for the selected date — empty before October 2021, rising to roughly 544 stocks by today. Users can open any stock for an explainable score breakdown with a regime-banded chart and five forward-return columns each paired with a colour-graded drawdown figure, sort every leaderboard, filter and search, and click any sample count to see the exact stored observations. They can save stocks to a watchlist and manage imports with live progress tracking. The Data Manager membership timeline and coverage diagnostic are restored pending live browser confirmation after the iteration-36 caching fix.
