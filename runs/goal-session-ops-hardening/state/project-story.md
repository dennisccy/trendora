# Project story so far

Trendora ranks stocks with explainable, evidence-checked signals — this chapter of its story is about making the running app itself trustworthy to operate day to day: fast to start, honest about its own state, unrestricted in the historical data it can pull in, and quick to load every page.

## How it has grown

Trendora's core analytics — the rankings, evidence-backed scoring, and every existing page — were already built and proven earlier. This chapter turned to running the app itself, starting from a stock-take that found backfills which silently did nothing, capped date ranges, calculations computed live on every visit, and no permanent crash log. Two early rounds closed the biggest of those gaps: backfills now pull in every requested day with no size cap, saying plainly when there's nothing new to do; heavy calculations now happen once, as new data arrives, restarts became near-instant, and the app genuinely enforces its memory limit with a permanent crash log.

A close look at real usage then surfaced two rough edges hiding in plain sight — an ordinary data update could briefly make the whole app look crashed, and a long job's on-screen progress could freeze and falsely claim to be stuck. A dedicated round fixed both: the status badge now tells the truth every time, showing a calm "Snapshot pending" message instead of a false crash alarm when new benchmark data is still processing, and the stuck-progress bug is fully gone.

With those fixed, the team turned to the last piece of this chapter: making sure every page loads quickly using only the data it actually needs. One round found and fixed a real slowdown on the Backtest page, which had been recalculating a large statistic from scratch on every visit — now it loads in well under a second by saving the calculation the moment new data arrives. That same round flagged the home page's small trend-history chart as still a bit too slow under everyday browser conditions.

The most recent round went after that chart, plus a matching slow spot on the data page's coverage calendar — both were competing with other requests for the backend's attention rather than being genuinely slow themselves. Both now load in about a second. While double-checking every page in the app, the team also chased down a scary-looking report that two other pages were taking minutes to load — that turned out to be a false alarm from testing under an overloaded machine, not a real problem, and was cleared up. The two features that had temporarily lost their up-to-date verification (the honest-startup status and the precompute-once behavior) were both freshly re-checked and confirmed still working. What's left before this chapter can be called finished: a couple of pages of internal documentation still describe the false-alarm slowdown as if it were real and need correcting, a rarely-visited results page can still be slow the very first time it's opened after new data lands, and the team still owes a couple of short recorded walkthroughs of the finished work.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse stock rankings, sector and theme views, backtests, research tools, and evidence-backed scores. An operator can back-fill any date range with no size cap, get an honest explanation when there's nothing new to add, and get a near-instant Data page after a restart, backed by clear startup/crash messaging and a genuinely enforced memory limit. The status badge can be trusted to describe what's really happening. The Backtest page's scorecard — once a 35-second wait — now appears in under a second, and the home page's trend chart and the data page's coverage calendar both now load in about a second in a real browser instead of occasionally stalling for a couple of seconds.
