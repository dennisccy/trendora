# Project story so far

Trendora ranks stocks with explainable, evidence-checked signals — this chapter of its story is about making the running app itself trustworthy to operate day to day: fast to start, honest about its own state, unrestricted in the historical data it can pull in, and quick to load every page.

## How it has grown

Trendora's core analytics were already built and proven earlier; this chapter turned to running the app day to day. Early rounds removed a hidden backfill cap, moved slow calculations to happen once as new data arrives instead of on every visit, made restarts near-instant, added a real enforced memory limit with a permanent crash log, and fixed rough edges where an ordinary data update could make the app look crashed or a progress bar could freeze and falsely claim to be stuck. A speed-tuning round then caught something bigger: two big data updates run back-to-back could briefly freeze the app for several minutes and force a manual restart — the team held that round back rather than call it finished, tracked the freeze to how the app handled running low on memory mid-calculation, fixed it, and proved the fix under the real worst case. A further round proved, by deliberately crashing the app mid-update, that an interrupted job's history now honestly shows the real progress it made rather than pretending nothing happened.

The round before this one re-checked page-loading speed now that startup itself had been hardened — mostly clean, but the Data page's list of index/benchmark data sources came back a bit over two seconds against its roughly one-and-a-half-second target, and the same rare background memory risk fired twice more in the background. Rather than call it finished, the team paused for extra scrutiny, then spent a full round just confirming that slow reading was genuinely real and not a busy-moment fluke.

This latest round fixed the slow spot for real: the Data page's list of index/benchmark sources and the home page's chart now load about ten times faster, by remembering the answer instead of recalculating it on every visit, confirmed on a real browser well under the target. But while testing that fix, the same rare background memory problem got dramatically worse — instead of a small, contained hiccup, it froze the entire app for about ten minutes with nothing responding, needing a manual restart to recover. Because a real product fix landed cleanly, but the memory risk just proved it can take the whole app down rather than just quietly stumble, the team is treating this round as a step backward rather than a finish line, and is holding the chapter open until the owner decides how to permanently fix or limit that risk.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse stock rankings, sector and theme views, backtests, research tools, and evidence-backed scores. It lets an operator back-fill any historical date range with no size cap and an honest zero-work explanation, keeps the status badge truthful during startup, a data update, or a crash, and leaves an honest record of real progress even when a mid-update crash happens. The Data page's list of index/benchmark sources and the home page's chart now also load quickly rather than sluggishly. One rare background memory risk — now shown capable of freezing the whole app for several minutes — still needs an owner decision before this chapter can be called complete.
