# Project story so far

Trendora ranks stocks with explainable, evidence-checked signals. This chapter tracks making the running app itself trustworthy day to day — fast, honest about its own state, and resilient under heavy background work.

## How it has grown

Early rounds removed a hidden backfill limit, moved slow calculations to run once at ingest, added a live status badge, and fixed a cluster of low-memory crashes — including cutting one calculation's peak memory from 7.8 GB to 3.1 GB (iteration 50). Iterations 51-57 chased a sometimes-silent status light, sped up the run-history list and the availability chart, and fixed a bug where a busy data-fetch job wrongly told users there was no data at all. Iteration 58 finished that honesty fix and corrected a wrong health-check record, but two journeys stayed unfinished — precomputing heavy numbers ahead of time, and keeping the service alive under a very heavy calculation — and, because some of that round's own test write-ups didn't match their raw measurements, the team switched back to the full, careful review process.

Iteration 59 delivered the project's best engineering yet on exactly those two problems: the Regime Lab research page can no longer crash under memory pressure (only the specific part that couldn't finish shows an honest "temporarily unavailable" note, while everything else keeps showing real numbers), and a real kill-and-restart test proved the Data page still shows correct saved numbers in well under a second afterward. Both checks ran live for the first time this project and both passed, with the server handling over seven hours of heavy load, including a 23-minute background job, with zero errors served. Even so, neither journey could be marked finished — the official checklist had a hole where these two journeys fell through, so nothing formally logged the passing result, and a promised recorded walkthrough still hadn't been made.

Iteration 60 started closing that checklist hole and finished the small fixes named the round before: the Regime Lab's opening calculation now recovers gracefully instead of crashing the page, and a Research-page table cell that can't be calculated right now shows a plain "Unavailable" notice instead of a misleading zero with a clickable link. The app had its best day yet — over 18 hours combined across these two rounds it answered thousands of health checks and finished an 18-minute data job with zero errors of any kind. But the team found a new, ordinary bug of its own: after that data job finished, the Data page kept showing yesterday's counts for nearly 48 minutes before catching up to the correct, already-saved numbers. That bug, plus one open owner question about how fast the app must respond to a health check during a long background job, are what's still keeping the last two journeys from closing. Today, six of the eight things the product promises work fully and are freshly re-verified; the last two work correctly in practice but are one small page fix and one owner decision away from being checked off.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse stock rankings, sector and theme views, backtests, and all five research tools with an honest "starting up"/"backend unavailable" status. It accepts a backfill over any date range with no hidden cap and explains clearly when there's nothing new to fetch, serves backtest results instantly from storage, and discloses when it's crunching numbers in the background. Pages load quickly because they only fetch what they need, and the Regime Lab page now keeps working even when the server is very busy, showing a plain "Unavailable" notice instead of a misleading zero for the specific part that couldn't be calculated in that moment.
