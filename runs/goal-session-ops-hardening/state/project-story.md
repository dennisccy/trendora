# Project story so far

Trendora ranks stocks with explainable, evidence-checked signals. This chapter tracks making the running app itself trustworthy day to day — fast, honest about its own state, and resilient under heavy background work.

## How it has grown

Early rounds removed a hidden backfill limit, moved slow calculations to run once at ingest, added a live status badge, fixed a cluster of low-memory crashes, restored a fuller review process, proved the Regime Lab page can no longer crash under memory pressure, and brought seven of the product's eight promises to fully passing after fixing a timezone mix-up. Iterations 62-69 chased an elusive slow-reply pattern during heavy background jobs, culminating in the first-ever completely unanswered health checks. Iteration 70 fixed the core idea — the app started preparing its status answer ahead of time instead of recalculating it on every check — and iteration 71 closed a related gap after a live re-test caught the status check going silent for two and a half minutes during a real data job, traced to the database running out of spare connections.

Iteration 72 fixed both root causes at once: the database's pool of spare connections was resized with a boot-time safety check, and the status check now always answers instantly with its last known value rather than pausing to recompute. But the resize also raised a new question — how much memory the app now uses with more than twice as many database connections open — on a machine with a history of memory crashes. Iteration 73 tried four times to answer that and could not finish: each attempt was interrupted, either by other work competing for the same shared computer or by running out of time just short of the heaviest calculation. Rather than guess, the team recorded a partial reading and left the last promise exactly where it stood: one measurement away from confirmed.

Iteration 74 finally got that measurement, on the first try, by joining two instruments the app already had (a memory sampler and its own internal timing logs) instead of needing one uninterrupted hour. During a real, heavy background job, the app's memory use peaked at 4,724 MB out of its 8,192 MB allowance — 42% to spare — and it kept answering its status check every second the whole time, the slowest answer taking under two seconds. That closes the eighth and final core promise: the app now has confirmed evidence that it stays responsive under its heaviest workload. Two smaller promises (about backtest storage and background-activity disclosure) are still resting on proof from two rounds ago, because the automated screenshot tool keeps serving broken, unstyled pages instead of real evidence — repairing that tool is next.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse stock rankings, sector and theme views, backtests, and all five research tools, always with an honest status message while the backend starts up. Backfills accept any date range with no hidden cap and explain when there's nothing new to fetch. Freshly calculated aggregates are ready right after a data import rather than computed on the fly, backtest results load instantly from storage, pages fetch only what they need, and the app discloses when it's crunching numbers in the background. All eight of the product's core promises, including staying responsive during its heaviest combined workloads, are now confirmed working.
