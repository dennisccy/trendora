# Project story so far

Trendora ranks stocks with explainable, evidence-checked signals. This chapter tracks making the running app itself trustworthy day to day — fast, honest about its own state, and resilient under heavy background work.

## How it has grown

Early rounds removed a hidden backfill limit, moved slow calculations to run once at ingest, added a live status badge, and fixed a cluster of low-memory crashes, cutting one calculation's peak memory from 7.8 GB to 3.1 GB. Iterations 51-57 fixed a sometimes-silent status light, sped up two slow pages, and stopped a busy data-fetch job from wrongly claiming there was no data. Iteration 58 fixed a wrong health-check record but left two journeys open, and the team returned to its full review process after some test write-ups stopped matching their raw measurements.

Iteration 59 delivered the project's best engineering yet on those two open journeys: the Regime Lab page can no longer crash under memory pressure — the one part that can't finish shows an honest "temporarily unavailable" note instead — and a real kill-and-restart test proved the Data page still shows correct saved numbers in under a second. Both checks passed live for the first time, with the server handling seven hours of heavy load with zero errors. Iteration 60 finished the small pieces from the round before and had the app's best day yet, finishing an 18-minute data job with zero errors — but it also mis-read a Data page number as stale, comparing a screenshot to a database entry using two different clocks, and held a journey back on that mistake.

Iteration 61 caught and corrected that mistake: the database stores its times in one timezone, and the app's own logs and screenshots use another, an hour apart — once accounted for, the Data page number was right all along. With that cleared up, the "aggregates are precomputed at ingest" journey moves to passing: seven of the product's eight promises now work and are freshly checked. The round also shipped a real improvement — the Data Manager page now refreshes its own numbers automatically about every 30 seconds, so a colleague's or script's backfill shows up without a manual reload. The health-check measurement for the one remaining journey, keeping the service alive during very heavy background work, was redone properly too: every check answered during a real 17-minute job, with only one answer running slightly over target. That last journey now waits on a single question for the product's owner: how fast must the app answer while a long job runs in the background?

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse stock rankings, sector and theme views, backtests, and all five research tools with an honest "starting up"/"backend unavailable" status. It accepts a backfill over any date range with no hidden cap, explains clearly when there's nothing new to fetch, serves backtest results instantly from storage, and discloses when it's crunching numbers in the background. Pages load quickly because they only fetch what they need. The Data Manager page now keeps its snapshot and gap counts current on its own, refreshing automatically within about 30 seconds of any backfill finishing — even one started elsewhere. The Regime Lab page keeps working under heavy server load, showing a plain "Unavailable" notice instead of a misleading zero for any part it can't calculate in the moment.
