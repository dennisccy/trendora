# Project story so far

Trendora ranks stocks with explainable, evidence-checked signals — this chapter of its story is about making the running app itself trustworthy to operate day to day: fast to start, honest about its own state, unrestricted in the historical data it can pull in, quick to load every page, and resilient enough that heavy background data work never takes the whole app down.

## How it has grown

Trendora's core analytics were already built and proven earlier; this chapter turned to running the app day to day. Early rounds removed a hidden backfill cap, moved slow calculations to happen once as data arrives rather than on every visit, made restarts near-instant, added a real enforced memory limit with a permanent crash log, and fixed rough edges where an ordinary data update could make the app look crashed or a progress bar could falsely freeze. A speed-tuning round then caught something bigger: two big data updates back-to-back could briefly freeze the whole app and force a manual restart — the team held that round back rather than call it finished, traced the freeze to how the app handled running low on memory mid-calculation, fixed it, and proved the fix under the real worst case. A further round proved, by deliberately crashing the app mid-update, that an interrupted job's history now honestly shows the real progress it made instead of pretending nothing happened.

A later round made two slow pages (the Data page's index/benchmark list and the home page's chart) load about ten times faster, confirmed on a real browser. But testing that fix uncovered something worse: the same rare background memory problem proved it could freeze the ENTIRE app for about ten minutes, not just stumble quietly — so the team called that round a step backward rather than a finish line, and paused for an owner decision on how to permanently fix it.

The owner authorized a direct fix, and this latest round delivered it: the background calculation behind two of those freezes was rewritten to use a fraction of the memory it used to need, proven with a real forced low-memory test and a real multi-request stress test, then confirmed against the actual full-size dataset — for the first time, the heaviest possible background calculation ran start to finish without freezing anything, comfortably under the app's memory limit. The team also proved, with a real scheduled crash-and-restart, that the "is it working?" status badge keeps telling the truth through a crash. One rough edge turned up while testing all this: opening the Backtest page during that same heavy background work can still take a few minutes to show results (it never errors or freezes, just a wait) — the next thing to chase down, alongside a couple of decisions only the product owner can make (a recorded walkthrough video, and whether the freeze-fix needs one more, tougher stress test).

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse stock rankings, sector and theme views, backtests, research tools, and evidence-backed scores. It lets an operator back-fill any historical date range with no size cap and an honest zero-work explanation, keeps the status badge truthful through startup, a data update, or a crash, and leaves an honest record of real progress even when a mid-update crash happens. The Data page's list of index/benchmark sources and the home page's chart load quickly rather than sluggishly. The app is now far more resistant to the kind of heavy background work that used to freeze it entirely, though the team isn't calling this fully finished until a slow spot opening the Backtest page during that same heavy work is tracked down and a couple of owner decisions are settled.
