# Project story so far

Trendora ranks stocks with explainable, evidence-checked signals. This chapter tracks making the running app itself trustworthy day to day — fast, honest about its own state, and resilient under heavy background work.

## How it has grown

Early rounds removed a hidden backfill limit, moved slow calculations to run once at ingest, added a live status badge, fixed a cluster of low-memory crashes, and brought seven of the product's eight core promises to passing after fixing a timezone mix-up. A multi-round hunt for slow health-check replies during heavy background jobs ended with two root causes found and fixed: the app now pre-computes its status answer instead of recalculating it live, and a database connection-pool shortage that once caused a silent gap was closed. A later round confirmed the eighth and final core promise stayed safe under real heavy load.

A stretch of rounds was then dogged by a screenshot tool that kept serving broken, unstyled pages instead of real evidence, voiding proof for two of the eight promises. Clean screenshots eventually returned and the two uncertain promises were re-verified — but no developer worked that round, so the glitch behind the broken screenshots stayed undiagnosed, just quiet.

The next round explained why, and it turned out to be bigger than one glitch: a safety rule built into the automation itself was skipping programming work whenever every one of the eight promises was already confirmed working — which, by then, was always true. The team used the loop's own escape hatch to force a round to actually staff a developer again, while re-confirming the two previously-uncertain promises with numbers checked directly against the database.

This latest round is the payoff of that escape hatch: a developer worked again, for the first time in three rounds, and shipped real, tested changes. The status badge and the green "board is current" strip now tell you how fresh that reading is (an "as of Ns ago" note), and a display bug that could hide the green "Ready" pill on smaller screens during background work is fixed. Along the way the team also found and fixed a more serious problem: a routine "does it still build" check could accidentally overwrite the running app and break it so it couldn't restart — caught and closed within the same round, though a related weak spot (an interrupted test leaving a similar landmine) is flagged but not yet fully guarded against. All eight core promises are still confirmed passing, each re-checked with fresh evidence this round, and the app handled a very heavy stretch of background work without a single error. The round's own automatic finishing check failed, though — not because anything broke, but because the report confirming three of the new features were tested never got updated with the passing results, so the team must fix that bookkeeping gap before the round can be called fully closed.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse stock rankings, sector and theme views, backtests, and all five research tools, always with an honest status message while the backend starts up. Backfills accept any date range with no hidden cap and explain when there's nothing new to fetch. Freshly calculated aggregates are ready right after a data import rather than computed on the fly, backtest results load instantly from storage with a "refreshing" note while new numbers compute, pages fetch only what they need, and the app discloses when it's crunching numbers in the background and when it's done. New this round: the status badge and board-status strip now show how fresh their reading is, and the "Ready" indicator no longer hides itself on smaller screens during background work. All eight core promises are confirmed working, each re-verified with fresh evidence this round.
