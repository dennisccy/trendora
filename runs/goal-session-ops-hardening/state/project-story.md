# Project story so far

Trendora ranks stocks with explainable, evidence-checked signals. This chapter tracks making the running app itself trustworthy day to day — fast, honest about its own state, and resilient under heavy background work.

## How it has grown

Early rounds removed a hidden backfill limit, moved slow calculations to run once at ingest, added a live status badge, fixed a cluster of low-memory crashes, and brought all eight of the product's core promises to passing after fixing a timezone mix-up and a screenshot-tooling glitch that had briefly hidden two of them.

A later round uncovered something bigger than a glitch: a safety rule built into the automation itself was quietly skipping programming work whenever every one of the eight promises was already confirmed working — which, by then, was always true. The team forced a round to staff a developer again, which turned up and fixed a serious flaw: a routine "does it still build" check could silently overwrite the live app and leave it unable to restart. The round after that built on the same momentum — the status badge and board strip gained an "as of Ns ago" freshness note, a display bug that hid the "Ready" pill on smaller screens was fixed, and a bug where a leftover test file could crash the app's launch was found and fixed within the same round. But that round's own closing checklist failed, because three of the freshly tested features never got their passing results merged into the official record, leaving it marked unfinished.

This latest round closed that gap and then some: the launcher now permanently defends itself by automatically deleting leftover test files before it builds, the freshness note now ticks up every second instead of freezing between checks, and the walkthrough picture that was supposed to show background computation in progress finally shows it. All eight core promises are confirmed working again, each re-checked against the app's own database this round, and the app handled a heavy stretch of background work without a single error. But the round again ended marked "blocked" — this time over a stray quoted word its own automatic checker mistook for an unfinished placeholder — and the team has now paused the whole project on purpose: for three rounds running, every promise has passed and nothing has broken, yet the process can't agree on when to call the job finished, because its own list of small clean-up notes keeps growing faster than it shrinks. The team is waiting for the owner to decide: call it done now and hand over the remaining notes as a to-do list, or spend a couple more short rounds tidying first.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse stock rankings, sector and theme views, backtests, and all five research tools, always with an honest status message while the backend starts up. Backfills accept any date range with no hidden cap and explain when there's nothing new to fetch. Freshly calculated aggregates are ready right after a data import rather than computed on the fly, backtest results load instantly from storage with a "refreshing" note while new numbers compute, pages fetch only what they need, and the app discloses when it's crunching numbers in the background and when it's done. The status badge and board-status strip show how fresh their reading is, counting up every second rather than freezing between checks, and the app's launcher now defends itself against a known way a leftover test file could stop it from starting at all. All eight core promises are confirmed working, each re-verified with fresh evidence this round.
