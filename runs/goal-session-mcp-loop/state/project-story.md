# Project story so far

Trendora is a market-leadership ranking tool that shows traders which scoring signals have genuinely earned their confidence labels — through rigorous out-of-sample testing, not in-sample curve-fitting.

## How it has grown

The first eighteen iterations built the trust layer — honest "Proven"/"Not yet proven" badges, an auditable evidence page, a private testing ground, and six certified trading edges — then deepened the price history to thirty years and widened the tracked company list to several hundred names. Every previously-certified edge then honestly failed re-testing on that deeper data (the system working as intended, not a step backward), and a brief crash that surfaced along the way was fixed the next round, with a safety net added against blank-screen errors.

Iterations 20-21 widened the Data Manager's price-refresh to the whole company list, gave its coverage calendar two clearly separate colors, and confirmed on the live site that it truly works.

Iteration 22 added deep historical market context to the dashboard's main chart — the S&P 500, Nasdaq, and Dow now reach back to 1996, three decades instead of one, alongside a volatility gauge and an economic-indicator line, each honestly disclosing its data provider. Partway through, the team fixed a bug that kept this deep history hidden off-screen unless someone scrolled far back manually, but the project's own formal double-check hadn't been re-run against the fix yet, so the round ended one step short of calling the feature finished.

Iteration 23 closed that gap: a dedicated, hands-on recheck in a real browser confirmed the three decades of history now show automatically the instant the dashboard loads, every line's data source is disclosed correctly, and nothing else broke — including a fresh recheck of the Data Manager's coverage calendar, last verified back in iteration 21. The feature from iteration 22 is now fully signed off rather than provisional; the only loose end was a narrow, invisible testing gap in one rarely-run internal check, unrelated to anything users see, which was fixed on the spot. The team is now deciding what to tackle next: proving a fresh trading signal on the newer, deeper data, or making the app faster.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse a leaderboard of hundreds of companies with an honest "proven" or "not yet proven" status on every score, drill into a fully auditable ledger of every trading idea tested so far, and view up to thirty years of price history for any stock in either a recent or full view. It shows a dashboard chart spanning three decades of S&P 500, Nasdaq, and Dow history plus a volatility gauge and a rate-spread indicator, each labeled with its data source, and a Data Manager page with a clear, color-coded calendar of data availability across the whole company list — degrading honestly, never with a blank screen, if something goes wrong.
