# Project story so far

Trendora is a market-leadership ranking tool that shows traders which scoring signals have genuinely earned their confidence labels — through rigorous out-of-sample testing, not in-sample curve-fitting.

## How it has grown

Trendora spent its first eighteen iterations building a trust layer of honest "Proven"/"Not yet proven" badges, an auditable evidence ledger, and several certified trading edges — then deepened its price history to thirty years, honestly retiring every old certified edge (the system working as designed). Later iterations widened data coverage across the whole company list, added decades of index history to the dashboard, and weathered two memory-crash scares in the background data jobs, each time pausing for a human check before shipping a verified fix.

Iteration 28 was a check-in: the team re-tested every pre-approved trading idea still labeled "not yet proven" on the deeper history, and none held up — a clean, honest dead end. The product owner then accepted the five ideas as honestly tested either way, and added nine new capabilities to the roadmap: a visible statistics budget, a graveyard of failed ideas, a daily health check, a live-data drift guard, a testing self-check, a watchlist concentration view, a risk card, and a drawdown-expectations panel. Iteration 29 banked that re-scope with zero code changes.

Iteration 30 built the roadmap's foundation: a pre-registration registry, so the system now refuses to test any brand-new idea unless it was written down and registered first.

Iteration 31 built directly on that foundation: a browsable graveyard of every idea the system has ever rejected, for the first time including rejections from its internal early-research track. Each entry shows exactly why it failed, the one permanently-retired idea is clearly flagged, and a plain-language rule explains when (if ever) a rejected idea could be re-tried. A small navigation rough edge — a link that didn't scroll to the right spot — was found and fixed along the way. Next up: a page showing the statistical testing "budget" still available, then a daily health check, a live-data drift guard, a testing self-check, and three risk/concentration views.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse a leaderboard of hundreds of companies with an honest "proven" or "not yet proven" status on every score, open a fully auditable evidence ledger for every trading idea ever tested, and view up to thirty years of price history for any stock. It shows a dashboard chart spanning three decades of index history plus a volatility gauge and a rate-spread indicator, and a Data Manager page with a color-coded coverage calendar — the heaviest data-refresh job now runs reliably. Users can browse the complete history of every idea the system has registered, tested, or rejected, and the system now refuses to test anything that wasn't registered first.
