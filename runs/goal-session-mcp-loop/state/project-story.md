# Project story so far

Trendora is a market-leadership ranking tool that shows traders which scoring signals have genuinely earned their confidence labels, through rigorous out-of-sample testing rather than in-sample curve-fitting.

## How it has grown

Early rounds built the trust layer — honest "proven"/"not yet proven" labels, a fully auditable evidence record, thirty years of price history, and wider stock coverage, surviving two fixed data-crash scares. By iteration 29 five of the platform's oldest trading ideas had honestly failed re-testing on the deeper history; iterations 30-37 then built a governance cluster — a pre-registration registry, a rejected-ideas graveyard, a testing-budget panel, and a daily trust banner watching for live-data drift and its own reliability.

Iteration 38 added a Watchlist "Concentration X-ray" (which stocks move together, how many independent bets a saved list really represents); iteration 39 was a pure re-verification round; iteration 40 added a "Risk budget" card to every stock's page (volatility, overnight-gap risk, worst losing stretch, room left before the bullish case breaks down) plus matching leaderboard columns.

Iteration 41 finished the roadmap: opening any tested idea's own record now shows what following it has historically felt like to hold, broken down by the market mood at the time, each figure carrying an honest sample size. With that feature landing, all 25 originally-planned capabilities were confirmed working, but the evaluator held back final sign-off pending one last verification pass.

Iteration 42 was that pass — a housekeeping-only round that re-ran every one of the 25 capabilities through an automated checker, re-measured page speed and memory use, and double-checked that no idea had been marked "proven" without real evidence behind it. A few automated checks flagged mismatches at first; each one traced back to a stale test recording, not a real problem, and the recordings were corrected. With a clean bill of health across the board, the project has now reached everything it originally set out to do.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse a leaderboard of hundreds of companies with an honest "proven" or "not yet proven" status on every score, open a fully auditable record behind any tested idea, and view up to thirty years of sourced price and macro history. It lets them browse every trading idea the system has tried or rejected, see the remaining testing budget, and check a shared trust banner for data freshness. On the Watchlist page it shows how concentrated a saved list is and how many independent bets it represents; on any stock's page it shows a "how much can this hurt" breakdown — volatility, overnight-gap risk, the worst historical rough patch, and distance from where the bullish case breaks down — plus matching leaderboard columns. And for any tested idea, it shows what holding it has historically felt like, phase by phase, with honest sample sizes throughout. Every one of these capabilities has now been independently re-verified end to end.
