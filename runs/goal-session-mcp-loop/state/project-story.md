# Project story so far

Trendora is a market-leadership ranking tool that shows traders which scoring signals have genuinely earned their confidence labels, through rigorous out-of-sample testing rather than in-sample curve-fitting.

## How it has grown

Early rounds built the trust layer: honest "proven" / "not yet proven" labels on every score, a fully auditable evidence record, thirty years of price history, and wider stock coverage — surviving two fixed data-crash scares along the way. By iteration 29, five of the platform's oldest trading ideas had honestly failed re-testing on the deeper history; the owner accepted that outcome and queued nine new features. Iterations 30 through 37 built those into a governance cluster — a pre-registration registry, a rejected-ideas graveyard, a testing-budget panel, and a daily trust banner that watches for live-data drift and checks its own reliability.

Iteration 38 added a Watchlist "Concentration X-ray" showing which saved stocks move together, how they cluster, and how many genuinely independent bets the list represents. Iteration 39 was a pure re-verification round, confirming all twenty-one built areas still worked with no new features added.

Iteration 40 added a "Risk budget" card to every stock's page — its volatility, overnight price-jump risk, worst losing stretch on record, and how much room is left before the case for holding it breaks down, each ranked against the market — plus matching sortable leaderboard columns and new glossary entries explaining each number. The figures were independently hand-checked against raw price data and matched exactly. The automated browser checker hit a technical outage partway through this round, but an earlier check the same day did see the card rendering correctly with real numbers, so the feature is confirmed working. One view now remains on the roadmap — a drawdown and rough-patch outlook — after which every currently planned feature will be built; before that, the browser-checker outage needs a quick look.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse a leaderboard of hundreds of companies with an honest "proven" or "not yet proven" status on every score, open a fully auditable record behind any tested idea, and view up to thirty years of price history with sourced index and macro context. Users can browse every idea the system has planned, tested, or rejected, see how much statistical testing budget remains, and check a shared trust banner for data freshness and the testing checker's own reliability. On the Watchlist page, users can see how concentrated their saved list is and how many genuinely independent bets it represents. On any stock's page, users can now also see a "how much can this hurt" breakdown — volatility, overnight-gap risk, worst historical rough patch, and distance from where the case for holding it breaks down — each ranked against the whole market, with matching columns on the leaderboard.
