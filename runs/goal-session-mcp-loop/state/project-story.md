# Project story so far

Trendora is a market-leadership ranking tool that shows traders which scoring signals have genuinely earned their confidence labels — through rigorous out-of-sample statistical testing, not in-sample curve-fitting.

## How it has grown

Trendora began this session with its analytical machinery already in place — Leadership, Entry Quality, and Risk scores; a market-regime tracker; sector leaderboards; a research lab — but with no user-facing evidence surface. The first two iterations built that surface: "Proven" and "Not yet proven" chips appeared on every score, a dedicated Evidence ledger page went live, and Leadership became the first signal to earn a certified label (+6.36% vs. SPY). Iterations 3 through 8 deepened the evidence layer: a Breakout-watch regime edge was certified (+6.12% in strong-market conditions), and the volatility-contraction pattern earned its first "Proven" badge in the Research Factor Lab at the 20-day horizon (+3.33%).

Iterations 9 and 10 introduced a disciplined private practice ledger running under stricter multiple-testing rules — a safe space to test ideas without inflating the canonical bar. The first sweep found that the volatility-contraction pattern also held at the 60-day horizon (+8.91% holdout edge). Iteration 11 promoted that winner to the canonical ledger and evolved the Factor Lab into a per-horizon strip, so users can see certified edges at both the 20-day and 60-day windows.

Iteration 12 ran three pre-approved two-factor pairs through the strict out-of-sample referee. Two failed honestly. The third — momentum leaders (relative strength vs. SPY) that are also near their 52-week high — passed with comfortable margin. Iteration 13 built the display: a combination lab badge that reads "Proven" only for that certified pair and "Not yet proven" for everything else, plus a new 6th row on the Evidence page with the full backing details and a +4.69% holdout edge. A minor anchor-scroll fix was committed at the end of iter-13 and needed a dedicated verification pass.

Iteration 14 was that final verification pass. The stack was brought up clean, the browser walked through the full certified selection flow, and the committed scroll fix was confirmed live: clicking the "Proven" badge deep-links to the Evidence page and the 6th combination row scrolls cleanly into view. Every number on screen matched the sealed ledger byte-for-byte. All eight Must-have journeys are now passing, and the goal is achieved.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse the stock leaderboard and see "Proven" or "Not yet proven" on every score; expand the proof panel on any Leadership card to read the sealed out-of-sample evidence (+6.36% vs. SPY); confirm Entry Quality and Risk are honestly labeled "not yet proven"; follow the Breakout-watch setup's certified edge in strong-market conditions; browse the Evidence page with six certified claims and round-trip links to each research surface; see the volatility-contraction pattern marked "Proven" in the Research Factor Lab at both the 20-day and 60-day horizons; use the Multi-factor combination lab to compose two-factor strategies and see an honest evidence badge — "Proven" for the certified momentum-and-proximity-to-high pair (+4.69% vs. SPY), "Not yet proven" for everything else — with a deep-link to the matching Evidence row.
