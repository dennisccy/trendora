# Project story so far

Trendora is a market-leadership ranking tool that shows traders which scoring signals have genuinely earned their confidence labels — through rigorous out-of-sample statistical testing, not in-sample curve-fitting.

## How it has grown

Trendora began this session with its analytical machinery already in place — Leadership, Entry Quality, and Risk scores; a market-regime tracker; sector leaderboards; a research lab — but with no user-facing evidence surface. The first two iterations built that surface: "Proven" and "Not yet proven" chips appeared on every stock score, a dedicated Evidence ledger page went live, and Leadership became the first signal to earn a certified label (+6.36% vs. the market). Iterations 3 through 8 deepened the evidence layer: a Breakout-watch regime edge was certified (+6.12% in strong-market conditions), and the volatility-contraction pattern earned its first "Proven" badge in the Research Factor Lab at the 20-day horizon (+3.33%).

Iterations 9 and 10 introduced a private staging ledger running under stricter multiple-testing rules — a safe space to test ideas without inflating the canonical bar. The first sweep found that volatility contraction also held at the 60-day horizon (+8.91% holdout edge). Iteration 11 promoted that winner to the canonical ledger and evolved the Factor Lab into a per-horizon chip strip, so users can see certified edges at both the 20-day and 60-day windows for the same pattern. Iteration 12 ran three pre-approved two-factor combinations through the out-of-sample referee: two failed honestly, and the third — momentum leaders (relative strength vs. the market) that are also near their 52-week high — passed with comfortable margin.

Iteration 13 built the combination display: a Combination Lab badge that reads "Proven" only for that certified pair, plus a 6th row on the Evidence page (+4.69% holdout edge). Iteration 14 was a verification pass confirming the committed deep-link scroll fix live in the browser; all eight Must-have journeys passed and the goal was declared achieved.

Iteration 15 extended the product through the continuous-improvement loop. The 3-month relative-strength factor's 60-day hold was pre-certified by the referee gate and became the 7th canonical ledger entry (PASS, Bonferroni divisor 7, p=0.0004998, edge +21.34%). No application code was needed — the existing general display machinery lit the new "Proven" badge automatically once the ledger row was appended. The full pipeline ran at depth: 13/13 browser tests, 39/39 frontend unit tests, 14/14 backend tests, Review PASS, Audit PASS_WITH_GAPS, Closure PASS. The factor lab now shows certified edges at two horizons for two different factors (volatility-contraction at 20 and 60 days; relative-strength at 60 days), and the Evidence page lists seven auditable claims with full metadata and round-trip deep-links.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse the stock leaderboard and see "Proven" or "Not yet proven" on every score; read the proof behind a Leadership card (6.36% verified edge vs. the market); confirm Entry Quality and Risk are honestly labeled not yet proven; explore the Breakout-watch setup's certified regime edge in strong markets; audit all seven certified claims on the Evidence page — each with out-of-sample edge, market comparison, p-value, and registration date; see the volatility-contraction pattern marked "Proven" in the Research Factor Lab at both the 20-day and 60-day windows; use the Multi-factor Combination Lab to compose two-factor strategies and see "Proven" for the certified momentum-and-proximity-to-high pair (everything else reads "Not yet proven"); and see the 3-month relative-strength top-decile factor marked "Proven" at the 60-day horizon on both the factor lab and the Evidence page.
