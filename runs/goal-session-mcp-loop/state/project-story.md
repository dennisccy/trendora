# Project story so far

Trendora is a market-leadership ranking tool that shows traders which scoring signals have genuinely earned their confidence labels — through rigorous out-of-sample testing, not in-sample curve-fitting.

## How it has grown

Trendora's early iterations built a trust layer of honest "Proven"/"Not yet proven" badges and an auditable evidence ledger, deepened price history to thirty years, widened stock coverage, and survived two memory-crash scares in its data jobs. By iteration 29 its five oldest trading ideas had all honestly failed re-testing on the deeper history, so the owner accepted the plateau and added nine new roadmap items instead: a statistics-budget page, a rejected-ideas graveyard, a daily health check, a live-data drift guard, and three risk/concentration views.

Iteration 30 built the roadmap's foundation: a registry that refuses to test any new idea unless it's written down first. Iteration 31 added a browsable graveyard of every rejected idea; iteration 32 fixed its one loose end (a lineage link that didn't scroll into view) and shipped a certification-budget panel. Iteration 33 delivered the roadmap's daily health check — one shared status strip on every page, quietly green on a normal day or an unmissable amber/red banner naming the exact problem, always spelling out serious problems as "do not rely on today's board" — leaving one loose end: a handful of older pages needed a quick re-check to confirm the new banner hadn't disturbed them.

Iteration 34 closed that loose end with a dedicated verification pass — no new features, just a thorough, automated re-check confirming that everything built so far still works exactly as intended: the leaderboard, the evidence ledger, the price history, the registry, the graveyard, the budget panel, and the daily status strip all checked out clean. Next up: a live-data drift watchdog that plugs directly into the daily status strip, followed by a handful of remaining features — a self-check for the testing system itself, a watchlist concentration view, and two per-stock risk views — before the roadmap is complete.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse a leaderboard of hundreds of companies with an honest "proven" or "not yet proven" status on every score, open a fully auditable evidence ledger for any trading idea, and view up to thirty years of price history plus decades of index and macro context for any stock — all while its data-refresh pipeline stays fast even on the heaviest job. Users can browse the complete history of every idea the system has registered, tested, or rejected, with working links back to each rejection's original registration, see how much of the statistical testing budget has been spent, and check one shared status strip on every page for whether today's board is safe to rely on.
