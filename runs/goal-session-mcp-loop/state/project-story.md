# Project story so far

Trendora is a market-leadership ranking tool that shows traders which scoring signals have genuinely earned their confidence labels — through rigorous out-of-sample statistical testing, not in-sample curve-fitting.

## How it has grown

Trendora began able to rank the market with no way to show which signals had actually earned traders' trust; over its first fifteen iterations it added honest "Proven" / "Not yet proven" badges, an auditable Evidence page, a private staging ground for testing new ideas safely, and six certified edges — reaching all nine original must-have capabilities, verified, by iteration 15.

With that milestone reached, the operator set a bigger ambition: swap today's roughly 5-year price history for a full ~30-year history across far more companies, so future charts, backtests, and proofs can draw on decades of data — a high-stakes swap the team is staging carefully in safe steps.

Iteration 16 built and tested a tool to fetch 30 years of history for every tracked company and hundreds more, staged safely out of sight. The fetch itself was refused by the outside data provider from this location — nothing was faked to cover the gap; the team paused for an operator decision instead of guessing.

The operator resolved that pause using a local archive of the same data already on the machine, then asked for one more thing before the switch-over: matching stock-market index and volatility history alongside the price data, each honestly labeled with where it came from. Iteration 17 delivered it — the S&P 500, Nasdaq-100, and Dow Jones indexes now reach back to 1996 from that archive, a volatility gauge was pulled fresh and deep from the source already trusted for it (matching today's numbers exactly on every overlapping day), and the three macro indicators already on screen were carried over unchanged. The deeper dataset is now proven to cover everything today's version does, clearing the last checkbox before the one-time switch-over — which can now proceed without waiting on another human decision.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse the stock leaderboard and see "Proven" or "Not yet proven" on every score, read the proof behind a Leadership score, confirm Entry Quality and Risk are honestly marked not yet proven, explore the Breakout-watch setup's certified regime edge, audit all seven certified claims on the Evidence page, see the volatility-contraction pattern proven at two holding periods, check the certified two-factor pair in the Combination Lab, and see the relative-strength factor proven at a 60-day horizon. The deeper 30-year history is fully staged behind the scenes, ready for the next iteration's one-time switch-over and a fresh, honest re-check of every "Proven" claim against the new data.
