# Project story so far

Trendora is a market-leadership ranking tool that shows traders which scoring signals have genuinely earned their confidence labels — through rigorous out-of-sample testing, not in-sample curve-fitting.

## How it has grown

Trendora's first eighteen iterations built a trust layer of honest "Proven"/"Not yet proven" badges and an auditable evidence ledger, deepened price history to thirty years, widened stock coverage, and survived two memory-crash scares in its data jobs. By iteration 28 its five oldest trading ideas had been honestly re-tested on the deeper history and all still came up short — a clean dead end the owner accepted at iteration 29, adding nine roadmap items: a statistics-budget page, a rejected-ideas graveyard, a daily health check, a live-data drift guard, and three risk/concentration views.

Iteration 30 built the roadmap's foundation: a registry that refuses to test any new idea unless it was written down first. Iteration 31 added a browsable graveyard of every rejected idea, including internal early-research rejections. Iteration 32 closed that round's loose end (a "jump to this idea's record" link that didn't scroll into view) and shipped a certification-budget panel showing how much testing rigor has been spent and how much remains.

Iteration 33 delivered the roadmap's daily health check: every page now carries one shared traffic-light verdict — quietly green on a normal day, or an unmissable amber or red banner naming the exact problem (stale data, a missing record file) when something needs attention. It works and was checked thoroughly across every required page in the browser. Before this round is fully signed off, the team still needs to re-confirm a handful of older pages weren't disturbed by the new banner — a quick, low-risk check, not a sign of anything broken. Next up: a live-data drift watchdog, or a self-check on the testing process itself for bias.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse a leaderboard of hundreds of companies with an honest "proven" or "not yet proven" status on every score, open a fully auditable evidence ledger for every trading idea ever tested, and view up to thirty years of price history plus decades of index and macro context for any stock — all while its data-refresh pipeline stays fast even on the heaviest job. Users can browse the complete history of every idea the system has registered, tested, or rejected, with working links back to each rejected idea's original registration, and see at a glance how much of its statistical testing budget has been spent. Every page also now carries one shared status strip showing whether today's board is safe to rely on.
