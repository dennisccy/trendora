# Project story so far

Trendora is a market-leadership ranking tool that shows traders which of their scoring signals have genuinely earned their confidence labels — through rigorous out-of-sample statistical testing, not in-sample curve-fitting.

## How it has grown

Trendora began this session with its analytical machinery already in place — Leadership, Entry Quality, and Risk scores; a market-regime tracker; sector leaderboards; a research lab; a watchlist — but with no user-facing evidence surface. Iterations 1 and 2 built that surface from scratch: "Proven" / "Not yet proven" chips appeared on every score across the leaderboard and stock detail pages, a dedicated Evidence ledger page went live, and Leadership became the first signal to earn a certified label after a sealed out-of-sample test (holdout edge +6.36% vs SPY, p ≈ 0.0005).

Iterations 3 through 6 closed the remaining gaps while the harness was refined. A second edge was certified in the Breakout-watch setup's Risk-on regime (+6.12%), and four pipeline defects were repaired so that all five original journeys ran through the canonical automated walkthrough end-to-end for the first time. Iteration 7 was a pure verification pass, after which the improvement loop proposed extending the goal with a sixth journey. Iteration 8 delivered it: the Research factor lab's vcp_contraction pattern was put through the referee gate and earned PASS (holdout +3.33%, p = 0.01149), gaining a dedicated "Proven" badge and an Evidence ledger row linking back to the full audit trail.

With four certified claims now in the canonical ledger, every future trial permanently tightens the Bonferroni bar — so a sustained search for multi-horizon and multi-factor edges could eventually fail to certify anything. Iteration 9 solved this before opening the wider search. It introduced an internal "practice ledger" running a LORD++ online-FDR economy that earns back testing capacity whenever it finds a real edge. Exploration in this staging area does not touch the canonical bar at all. Nothing visible changed: the Evidence page, every badge, and all displayed numbers are byte-for-byte identical to iteration 8. Two new journeys — a certified multi-horizon edge (J-07) and a curated two-factor combination edge (J-08) — are the next targets, with the economy now in place to pursue them sustainably.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse 120 ranked stocks each showing a "Proven" or "Not yet proven" badge on every score; expand a "Why proven?" panel on any Leadership card to read the sealed out-of-sample proof (holdout edge, benchmark comparison, certification date); confirm that Entry Quality and Risk are honestly labeled "Not yet proven"; view all four certified claims on the Evidence page with round-trip links to the leaderboard, research lab, and event-study lab; follow the Market Regime card to see the Breakout-watch setup's certified edge in Risk-on conditions; and on the Research factor lab see vcp_contraction labeled "Proven" with a link to its full auditable record, while all others (including the tested-and-rejected ma_stack) honestly show "Not yet proven."
