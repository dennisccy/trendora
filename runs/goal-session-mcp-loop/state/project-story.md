# Project story so far

Trendora is a market-leadership ranking tool that shows traders which scoring signals have genuinely earned their confidence labels — through rigorous out-of-sample testing, not in-sample curve-fitting.

## How it has grown

Trendora's early iterations built a trust layer of honest "Proven"/"Not yet proven" badges and an auditable evidence ledger, deepened price history to thirty years, widened stock coverage, and survived two memory-crash scares in its data jobs. By iteration 29 its five oldest trading ideas had all honestly failed re-testing on the deeper history, so the owner accepted the plateau and queued nine new roadmap items instead.

Iterations 30 through 33 built that roadmap's governance cluster: a pre-registration registry that refuses to test any idea unless it's written down first, a browsable graveyard of every rejected idea, a certification-budget panel, and a daily trust status strip — quietly green on a normal day, an unmissable amber/red banner naming the problem on a bad one. Iteration 34 was a dedicated verification pass confirming everything built so far still worked exactly as intended, with no new features.

Iteration 35 plugged a live-data drift guard into that status strip: when a price refresh comes in from the outside world, the platform now quietly checks whether the freshly-pulled numbers agree with the trusted, already-saved history — something it had no way of noticing before. If a stock's history was silently revised (which can happen after a dividend or split), the Data Manager page now names the exact stock and dates affected, and the trust strip turns cautionary everywhere on the site until a clean refresh supersedes it. The build hit a rough patch mid-iteration when the team's own testing tools stopped responding, but every independent downstream check re-verified the work from scratch and it came back clean.

Three features remain before the roadmap is complete: a self-check for the testing system itself, a watchlist concentration view, and two per-stock risk views.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse a leaderboard of hundreds of companies with an honest "proven" or "not yet proven" status on every score, open a fully auditable evidence ledger for any trading idea, and view up to thirty years of price history plus index and macro context for any stock. Users can browse the complete history of every idea the system has registered, tested, or rejected, see how much of the statistical testing budget has been spent, and check one shared trust strip on every page — now also covering whether the live data itself has silently changed underneath them.
