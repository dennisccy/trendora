# Project story so far

Trendora is a market-leadership ranking tool that shows traders which scoring signals have genuinely earned their confidence labels — through rigorous out-of-sample statistical testing, not in-sample curve-fitting.

## How it has grown

Trendora began this session able to rank the market, but with no way to show which signals actually deserved traders' trust. Iteration by iteration it added "Proven" / "Not yet proven" badges on every score and an auditable Evidence page, then — after building a private staging ground to test new ideas safely before going public — certified six more edges: a market-regime edge, a volatility-contraction pattern proven at two holding periods, a two-factor combination, and a relative-strength edge at a longer holding period. By iteration 15, all nine original must-have capabilities were complete and independently verified.

With that milestone reached, the operator set a bigger ambition: swap today's roughly 5-year price history for a full ~30-year history from a free data provider, across many more companies, so future charts, backtests, and proofs can draw on decades of market data instead of just a few years. Because that swap is high-stakes — every existing "Proven" claim would need to be re-earned on the new data — the team split the work into safe stages, the same careful approach it used earlier when it built its private testing economy before widening its search for new edges.

Iteration 16 was the first of those stages: build and test a tool that fetches 30 years of history for every tracked company, and hundreds more, staged safely off to the side where users never see it, and checked thoroughly for correctness — no invented history, no split-adjustment glitches, no dates earlier than a company's real market debut. That tool was built and tested successfully, but when the team actually tried the download, the outside data provider refused it from this location — nothing was faked or substituted to paper over the gap. The block is fully documented, and the team has paused, awaiting an operator decision — a different network, an access key, or a different data source — before the next stage, swapping in the new data and re-proving every claim, can begin.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse the stock leaderboard and see "Proven" or "Not yet proven" on every score, read the proof behind a Leadership score, confirm Entry Quality and Risk are honestly marked not yet proven, explore the Breakout-watch setup's certified regime edge, audit all seven certified claims on the Evidence page, see the volatility-contraction pattern proven at two holding periods, see the certified two-factor pair proven in the Combination Lab, and see the relative-strength factor proven at a 60-day holding period. Nothing changed in what users can do this round — the team is quietly preparing a much deeper price history behind the scenes, on hold until an operator makes that call.
