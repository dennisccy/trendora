# Project story so far

Trendora is a market-leadership ranking tool that shows traders which scoring signals have genuinely earned their confidence labels — through rigorous out-of-sample testing, not in-sample curve-fitting.

## How it has grown

The first eighteen iterations built Trendora's trust layer — honest "Proven"/"Not yet proven" badges, an auditable evidence ledger, and several certified trading edges — then, when the team deepened the price history to thirty years across several hundred companies, every old certified edge honestly recomputed to failing (the system working as designed). The iterations that followed widened price-refresh to the whole company list, added three decades of index history to the dashboard, and worked through two serious memory-crash scares in the background data-refresh jobs — each time pausing for human review and shipping a verified fix rather than pushing through.

Iteration 28 was a check-in rather than a build round: the team re-tested every pre-approved trading idea still labeled "not yet proven" on the deeper history, and none of them held up — a clean, honest dead end, not a bug. Rather than keep re-testing closed ideas, the product owner made a call at that plateau: accept the five ideas as honestly tested either way (proven or honestly not-yet-proven, so long as the answer is truthful), and add nine new capabilities to the roadmap — a visible statistics budget, a browsable graveyard of failed ideas, a daily go/no-go health check, a live-data drift guard, a self-check on the testing process itself, a watchlist concentration view, a per-stock risk card, and a drawdown-expectations panel. Iteration 29 banked that re-scope cleanly with zero code changes.

Iteration 30 kicked off that new roadmap with its foundation piece: a pre-registration registry. The product now keeps a browsable record of every trading idea it has ever tried — what it claimed, why, when, and its outcome — and, just as important, the system now refuses to test any brand-new idea going forward unless it was written down and registered first. This closes the door on quietly testing an idea and only "discovering" it worked after the fact. The next pieces on the roadmap are a browsable graveyard of failed ideas and a visible statistics budget, followed by a daily health check, a live-data drift guard, a self-check on the testing process, and three risk/concentration views.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse a leaderboard of hundreds of companies with an honest "proven" or "not yet proven" status on every score, open a fully auditable evidence ledger for every trading idea ever tested, and view up to thirty years of price history for any stock in a recent or full view. It shows a dashboard chart spanning three decades of major-index history plus a volatility gauge and a rate-spread indicator, each labeled by source, and a Data Manager page with a color-coded availability calendar across the whole company list — the full-universe data refresh, the heaviest job in the app, now completes reliably without crashing the backend. Users can also browse the complete history of every trading idea the system has ever registered or tested, in one place, with historical entries clearly labeled.
