# Project story so far

Trendora is a market-leadership ranking tool that shows traders which scoring signals have genuinely earned their confidence labels — through rigorous out-of-sample testing, not in-sample curve-fitting.

## How it has grown

Early rounds built a trust layer of honest "proven" / "not yet proven" labels, a fully auditable evidence record, thirty years of price history, and wider stock coverage — surviving two data-job crashes along the way, both fixed and re-verified. By iteration 29, five of the platform's oldest trading ideas had honestly failed re-testing on the deeper history, so the owner accepted that outcome and queued nine new features instead.

Iterations 30 through 37 built out a governance cluster — a registry of every idea promised in advance for testing, a browsable graveyard of rejected ideas, a testing-budget panel, and a single daily trust banner on every page that also catches live prices drifting from validated history and checks whether the testing system's own checker can itself be trusted. A clean-up round in between confirmed everything still held.

Iteration 38 added a "Concentration X-ray" to the Watchlist page — showing which saved stocks actually move together, grouping them into clusters, and giving one headline number for how many genuinely independent bets the list represents, plus crowding by sector, theme, and signal. Real production data confirmed the underlying math to ten decimal digits, though the round's routine final check — confirming nothing else quietly broke — didn't finish in time.

Iteration 39 was exactly that missing check: a dedicated re-verification round with no new features at all. All twenty-one areas built so far — from the leaderboard's honest evidence badges through the governance cluster to the Concentration X-ray — were re-confirmed working end to end, closing out the previous round's paperwork gap. Nothing new appeared for users this time, but nothing broke either. Two views remain on the roadmap — a per-stock risk card and a drawdown/dry-spell outlook — after which every currently planned feature will be built.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse a leaderboard of hundreds of companies with an honest "proven" or "not yet proven" status on every score, open a fully auditable record for any tested idea, and view up to thirty years of price history with clearly sourced index and macro context. Users can browse every idea the system has planned, tested, or rejected, see how much of its statistical testing budget remains, and check one shared trust banner that watches for silent live-data drift and for whether the testing checker itself is trustworthy. On the Watchlist page, users can see how concentrated their saved list really is — which names move together, how they cluster, and how many genuinely independent bets it represents, plus sector, theme, and setup crowding.
