# Project story so far

Trendora is a market-leadership ranking tool that shows traders which scoring signals have genuinely earned their confidence labels, through rigorous out-of-sample testing rather than in-sample curve-fitting.

## How it has grown

Early rounds built the trust layer — honest "proven" / "not yet proven" labels on every score, a fully auditable evidence record, thirty years of price history, and wider stock coverage, surviving two fixed data-crash scares. By iteration 29 five of the platform's oldest trading ideas had honestly failed re-testing on the deeper history, and the owner queued nine new features, which iterations 30-37 built into a governance cluster: a pre-registration registry, a rejected-ideas graveyard, a testing-budget panel, and a daily trust banner watching for live-data drift and its own reliability.

Iteration 38 added a Watchlist "Concentration X-ray" (which saved stocks move together, how many independent bets the list represents); iteration 39 was a pure re-verification round; iteration 40 added a "Risk budget" card to every stock's page (volatility, overnight-gap risk, worst losing stretch, room left before the bullish case breaks down) plus matching leaderboard columns, confirmed working through a same-day browser-checker outage via independently hand-checked figures.

Iteration 41 built the last unbuilt feature on the roadmap: opening any tested idea's record now shows what following it has historically felt like to hold, broken down by the market mood at the time — typical and worst-case losing depth, days spent underwater, time to recover, and the longest losing streak on record, each with an honest sample size and a "not enough history yet" label wherever the sample is too thin. A same-day loading slowdown was caught and fixed before it reached anyone but the testers. Every quality check this round came back clean and the new panel is confirmed working; the only step left is a housekeeping round that double-checks everything already built still works together, after which every currently planned feature will be complete.

## What it can do today

The product lets users browse a leaderboard of hundreds of companies with an honest "proven" or "not yet proven" status on every score, open a fully auditable record behind any tested idea, and view up to thirty years of price history with sourced index and macro context. Users can browse every idea the system has planned, tested, or rejected, see how much testing budget remains, and check a shared trust banner for data freshness and its own reliability. On the Watchlist page, users can see how concentrated their saved list is and how many independent bets it represents. On any stock's page, users can see a "how much can this hurt" breakdown — volatility, overnight-gap risk, worst historical rough patch, and distance from where the case for holding it breaks down — with matching leaderboard columns. And now, opening any tested idea's own record, users can read what following it has historically felt like to hold, phase by phase, with honest sample sizes throughout.
