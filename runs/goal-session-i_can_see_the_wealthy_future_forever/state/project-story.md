# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner — a decision-support workstation that ranks the market after the close and earns the user's trust with forward-tested evidence rather than ever shouting "buy."

## How it has grown

Trendora arrived already substantially built — a dark dashboard ranking US stocks, sectors, and themes after the close, every score explained and every idea showing what would prove it wrong. Early passes closed the last gaps — one shared date control, click-through from any forward-tested return to the names behind it, a Data Manager that backfills without inventing numbers, scores identical across pages, a watchlist that survives a restart, and fast loads from saved snapshots — completing all nineteen original must-have journeys.

The owner then set a larger target: a bigger universe, multiple chart timeframes, and research labs. Charts learned to keep drawing past a chosen past date all the way to today (the later stretch greyed out and clearly marked) while scores still reflect only what was known then, and a backtest view began showing the real return each top sector, theme, and ranked stock delivered, with one control that flips every return between time horizons at once. Widening the universe toward roughly 500 names then stalled across several passes — the machinery is built and tested, but the one-time download of real price and market-value history it needs kept hitting a temporary outage of the free data source, and the team refused to fake it. So work turned to tools that run on data already collected: the scanner learned to spot three chart patterns instead of one, and a Research area opened with a Factor Lab that tests whether a chosen signal actually sorted future returns — ten ranked groups, each with its average and a downside-only risk-adjusted return, plus a score of how well the signal lines up with the future. Later passes split that evidence by market mood, then let you combine two or three signals at once to see whether they beat each signal alone and the whole market.

This latest pass made volatility a first-class family. You can now test four volatility measures — overall price swing, historical volatility, whether volatility is drying up, and downside-only volatility — each with the same ten-group, rank-correlation, risk-adjusted, and by-market-mood evidence, gathered under one "Volatility" heading and reported honestly even when the finding is that volatility carried no real edge in this universe. The new measures changed no stock's score or ranking. Next the Lab will study how every past occurrence of a setup or pattern actually played out — its typical gain, its worst dip along the way, and the best holding period.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see the day's market at a glance; browse ranked stocks, sectors, and themes and filter the stock list by sector, setup, or any of three chart patterns; open any stock for a plain-English scorecard — identical on the list and detail page — plus the price that would prove the idea wrong; rewind the whole app to any past day with one shared date control and watch a chart continue past it; read forward-tested evidence of how past picks performed by stock, sector, and ranking tier; open the Research area's Factor Lab to test whether a signal sorts future returns — by decile and rank correlation, raw and downside-risk-adjusted, split by market regime, combined two or three at a time, and now across a volatility family of four measures; save a watchlist that survives a restart; grow the dataset by date or range; and look up every label and pattern in a plain-language glossary — always with honest "not enough data yet" marks instead of fabricated numbers.
