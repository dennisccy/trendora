# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner — a decision-support workstation that ranks the market after the close and earns the user's trust with forward-tested evidence rather than ever shouting "buy."

## How it has grown

Trendora arrived already substantially built — a dark dashboard ranking US stocks, sectors, and themes after the close, every score explained and every idea showing what would prove it wrong. Early passes closed the last gaps — one shared date control, click-through from any forward-tested return to the names behind it, a Data Manager that backfills without inventing numbers, scores identical across pages, a watchlist that survives a restart, and fast loads from saved snapshots — completing all nineteen original must-have journeys.

The owner then set a larger target: a bigger universe, multiple chart timeframes, and research labs. Charts learned to keep drawing past a chosen past date all the way to today (the later stretch greyed out and clearly marked) while scores still reflect only what was known then, and a backtest view showed the real return each top sector, theme, and ranked stock delivered, with one control that flips every return between time horizons at once. Widening the universe toward roughly 500 names stalled across several passes — the machinery is built and tested, but the one-time download of real price history it needs kept hitting a temporary outage of the free data source, and the team refused to fake it. So work turned to tools that run on data already in hand: the scanner learned three chart patterns instead of one, and a Research area opened with a Factor Lab that tests whether a chosen signal actually sorted future returns — later passes split that evidence by market mood, let you combine two or three signals at once, and gathered four volatility measures under one heading, each reported honestly even when the finding was "no real edge here."

This latest pass added a Setup & Pattern Lab that studies how every past occurrence of a setup or chart pattern actually played out. Pick "breakout watch" or "pullback to a rising average" and read, pooled across every past day with data, its typical gain, how often it finished positive, the worst dip and best rise along the way, the best holding length, and how it split by market mood and sector — always with honest "not enough history" marks (the rare default shows a count of two rather than inventing numbers). No stock's score changed. Next the product will tie it together: start from a piece of lab evidence and travel straight to the live names behind it.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see the day's market at a glance; browse ranked stocks, sectors, and themes and filter the list by sector, setup, or any of three chart patterns; open any stock for a plain-English scorecard — identical on list and detail — plus the price that would prove the idea wrong; rewind the whole app to any past day with one shared date control and watch a chart continue past it; read forward-tested evidence of how past picks performed by stock, sector, and ranking tier; explore the Research area to test whether a signal sorts future returns — by group, market mood, combinations, and a volatility family — and study any setup or pattern's full pooled track record, including its worst-dip / best-rise and best holding period; save a watchlist that survives a restart; grow the dataset by date; and look up every label in a plain-language glossary — always with honest "not enough data yet" marks instead of fabricated numbers.
