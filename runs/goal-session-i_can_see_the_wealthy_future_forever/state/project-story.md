# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner — a decision-support workstation that ranks the market after the close and earns the user's trust with forward-tested evidence rather than ever shouting "buy."

## How it has grown

Trendora arrived already substantially built — a dark dashboard ranking US stocks, sectors, and themes after the close, every score explained and every idea showing what would prove it wrong. Early passes closed the original gaps: one shared date control, click-through from any forward-tested return to the names behind it, a Data Manager that backfills without inventing numbers, identical scores across pages, a watchlist that survives a restart, and fast loads from saved snapshots — completing all nineteen original must-have journeys.

The owner then set a larger target: a bigger universe, more chart timeframes, and research labs. Charts learned to keep drawing past a chosen past date all the way to today while scores still reflect only what was known then. Widening the universe toward roughly 500 names stalled repeatedly — the one-time download of real price history it needs kept hitting an outage of the free data source, and the team refused to fake it — so work turned to data already in hand: the scanner learned three chart patterns, then a Research area opened with a Factor Lab that tests whether a signal actually sorted future returns, later split by market mood, combined across signals, extended to a family of volatility measures, and rounded out by a Setup & Pattern Lab studying every past occurrence of a setup or pattern — always with honest "not enough history" marks. A synthesis capstone then let you travel from any lab finding straight to the leaderboard filtered to those names and on to a stock's full scorecard, with filtered views living in shareable links. With 28 of 31 targets passing and only the data-walled three left, the project paused for the owner.

The owner returned and re-scoped the goal: the three data-blocked items no longer halt the project, the historical track record was consolidated onto a single home, and two new research goals were added. This most recent pass delivered the first piece — the forward-tested evidence (how the rankings actually performed, by score grade, against the benchmarks, and versus a control group) moved off the separate "System Health" page onto the **Backtest** page, where you can now rewind it to any past date and watch the sample honestly shrink as you go further back; returning to today reproduces the full all-history numbers. System Health is gone, so all of this now sits under the single shared date control, and no stock's score changed. Next, the Research area will gain a combined multi-signal ranking and an all-history-versus-as-of time switch — after which the product is considered delivered, with the universe-size items recorded as honestly blocked.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see the day's market at a glance; browse ranked stocks, sectors, and themes and filter by sector, setup, or chart pattern via shareable links; open any stock for a plain-English scorecard identical on list and detail, plus the price that would prove the idea wrong; rewind the whole app to any past day with one shared date control and watch a chart continue past it; read forward-tested evidence by score grade, benchmark, and control group on the Backtest page — now also as of any past date; explore the Research area to test whether a signal sorts future returns by group, market mood, combination, and a volatility family, and study any setup or pattern's full track record; travel from a finding to the names expressing it and on to the scorecard; save a watchlist that survives a restart; grow the dataset by date; and look up every label in a plain-language glossary — always with honest "not enough data yet" marks instead of fabricated numbers.
