# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner — a decision-support workstation that ranks the market after the close and earns the user's trust with forward-tested evidence rather than ever shouting "buy."

## How it has grown

Trendora arrived already substantially built — a dark dashboard ranking US stocks, sectors, and themes after the close, every score explained and every idea showing what would prove it wrong. Early passes closed the original gaps: one shared date control, click-through from any forward-tested return to the names behind it, a Data Manager that backfills without inventing numbers, matching scores across pages, a restart-proof watchlist, and fast snapshot loads — finishing all nineteen original journeys.

The owner then asked for more. Charts learned to keep drawing past a chosen date to today while scores still reflected only what was known then. A Research section grew into a Factor Lab (split by market mood, blended across signals, extended to a volatility family), a Setup & Pattern Lab studying every past occurrence, and a synthesis capstone linking any finding to the filtered leaderboard and on to a stock's scorecard. A re-scope made the data-blocked items non-halting and gave Research its own all-history to as-of-date toggle. Real data import came next: a source picker with session-only API keys (never saved), a chunked import engine that pauses and resumes across restarts, and an Expand-universe job that screens a 548-name candidate pool and writes exactly who passed and who was omitted with the reason.

Iteration 24 rounded out the Coverage surface: every aggregate figure gained a plain-language definition, a sentence distinguishes "universe" from "symbols," and a per-symbol table lets the operator inspect every ticker at a glance. A seed-safe Remove-data panel was also built, with a confirm-preview that lists exactly the user-added bars to be deleted and the dependent snapshots that would cascade out before anything is touched — fully verified in source but still waiting for a clean browser capture.

Iteration 25 added two more self-service panels to the Data Manager. A "Missing-data diagnostic" panel now flags every universe member that has too little history or internal gaps — no-history, thin, or intra-series gaps — showing the exact shortfall and a one-click button that pulls only the missing bars through the existing chunked, checkpointed engine. A "Unfinished imports" panel unifies paused (rate-limited), partially-completed, and fully-failed imports in one place, each with a plain-language description and Resume, Retry, or Remove controls. Both panels are source- and test-proven with a 601-green backend suite, but their defining end-to-end browser flows are still waiting for a clean capture with a suitable fixture — the same final step needed for the Remove-data and Expand-universe flows carried over from earlier.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see the day's market at a glance; browse ranked stocks, sectors, and themes; open any stock for a plain-English scorecard; rewind the whole app to any past date; read forward-tested evidence on the Backtest page; explore Research labs by factor decile, market mood, signal blend, volatility family, event study, and all-history or point-in-time toggle; travel from any finding to the filtered leaderboard and on to a stock detail; keep a restart-proof watchlist; read every label in the glossary; import from a selectable data provider with a session-only key; run large imports in visible batches that pause and resume; grow the universe via the Expand job; read a labelled coverage panel with a per-symbol table; see a diagnostic panel that names every data gap in plain language; and manage every incomplete import in one unified panel with Resume, Retry, and Remove actions.
