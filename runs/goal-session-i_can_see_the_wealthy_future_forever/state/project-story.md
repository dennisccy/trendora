# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner — a decision-support workstation that ranks the market after the close and earns the user's trust with forward-tested evidence rather than ever shouting "buy."

## How it has grown

Trendora arrived at this session already substantially built — a dense, dark analytical dashboard that ranks US stocks, sectors, and themes after the close around a core of skepticism: every score is explained, every idea shows what would prove it wrong, and the product tracks whether its own past picks actually worked. Its first pass added nothing on purpose — a deliberate stock-taking that flagged three gaps to close.

The passes that followed closed them. The backtest screen moved onto the single shared date control, so picking a date once holds everywhere you go. Then any forward-tested return became something you can open up — into the individual stocks, sectors, and ranking tiers that drove it. The Data Manager arrived next: a screen to backfill a single date or a whole range and fetch fresh prices with a live progress bar, growing the track record while refusing to invent numbers on a failed fetch. A closing pass then confirmed scores read identically across pages, a saved watchlist survives a full restart, and the main screens load fast from saved snapshots — completing all nineteen original must-have journeys and meeting the first goal.

With the product complete, the owner set a larger target — a next wave of research journeys spanning a bigger universe, multiple chart timeframes, and a set of factor- and pattern-research labs. This latest pass opened that wave with its two lowest-risk pieces. Now, when you travel to a past date and open a stock, the price chart keeps drawing past that date all the way to today — the later stretch greyed out and clearly marked — so you can see what actually happened next, while the scores and pattern flags still reflect only what was known on the chosen day. And the backtest page now shows the real return each top sector, theme, and ranked stock went on to deliver, with one control that flips all of those returns between time horizons at once; anywhere the window hasn't elapsed, a dash appears instead of a fake number. Next up is widening the watched universe toward roughly 500 names so the evidence rests on a larger sample.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see the day's market overview at a glance; browse ranked lists of stocks, themes, and sectors and filter the stock list by sector, setup, or the VCP chart pattern; open any stock for an explained scorecard — identical on the list and its detail page — and the price that would invalidate the idea; revisit past scan days as recorded and move the whole product to any past day with one shared date control; read forward-tested evidence against the market and a fair random benchmark and break it down by stock, sector, and ranking tier; on a past date, watch the stock chart continue past that date to reveal what happened next (display-only); read the realized return each top sector, theme, and stock delivered at a chosen horizon on the backtest page; save a watchlist that survives a restart; grow the dataset on demand by date or range; and look up every label and pattern in a plain-language glossary — always with honest "not enough data yet" marks instead of fabricated numbers.
