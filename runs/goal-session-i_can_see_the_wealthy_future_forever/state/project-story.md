# Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner — a decision-support workstation that ranks the market after the close and earns the user's trust with forward-tested evidence rather than ever shouting "buy."

## How it has grown

Trendora arrived already substantially built — a dark dashboard ranking US stocks, sectors, and themes after the close, every score explained and every idea showing what would prove it wrong. Early passes closed the original gaps — one shared date control, click-through from any forward-tested return to the names behind it, a Data Manager that backfills without inventing numbers, identical scores across pages, a watchlist that survives a restart, and fast snapshot loads — completing all nineteen original must-have journeys.

The owner then set a larger target: a bigger universe, more chart timeframes, and research labs. Charts learned to keep drawing past a chosen date all the way to today while scores still reflect only what was known then. Widening the universe toward ~500 names stalled — the one-time download of real price history it needs kept hitting an outage of the free data source, and the team refused to fake it — so work turned to data already in hand: the scanner learned three chart patterns, then a Research area opened with a Factor Lab testing whether a signal actually sorted future returns (later split by market mood, combined across signals, and extended to a volatility family) plus a Setup & Pattern Lab studying every past occurrence — always with honest "not enough history" marks. A synthesis capstone then let you travel from any lab finding to the filtered leaderboard and on to a stock's scorecard.

The owner returned and re-scoped the goal: the data-blocked universe items no longer halt the project, two new research goals were added, and the historical track record was consolidated onto the Backtest page — rewindable to any past date, with System Health retired so everything sits under the single shared date control. This latest pass made the Research area's multi-signal combination genuinely useful: combining factors used to leave the headline "Combined" line almost always empty, because it demanded stocks meeting *every* signal at once. Now the app blends the chosen signals into one combined ranking that is reliably filled with real numbers, you can mix up to all eleven signals instead of three, and the strict "meets every signal" view stays as an honest secondary line marked "not enough names" when nothing qualifies. No stock's score changed. Next, the Research area gains an all-history-versus-as-of date switch — after which the product is delivered, with the universe-size items recorded as honestly blocked.

## What it can do today

The product lets users see the day's market at a glance; browse ranked stocks, sectors, and themes and filter by sector, setup, or chart pattern via shareable links; open any stock for a plain-English scorecard identical on list and detail, plus the price that would prove the idea wrong; rewind the whole app to any past day with one shared date control and watch a chart continue past it; read forward-tested evidence by score grade, benchmark, and control group on the Backtest page as of any past date; explore the Research area to test whether a signal sorts future returns by group, market mood, a populated multi-signal combination, and a volatility family, and study any setup or pattern's full track record; travel from a finding to the names behind it and on to the scorecard; save a watchlist that survives a restart; grow the dataset by date; and look up every label in a plain-language glossary — always with honest "not enough data yet" marks.
