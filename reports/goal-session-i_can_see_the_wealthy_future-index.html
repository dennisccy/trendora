The story so far

Project story so far

Trendora is a local-first, research-only US stock scanner that, after the market closes each day, ranks the market from overall mood down to individual stocks and explains every score it shows — it never just says "buy this."

How it has grown

It began from a deliberately empty starting line: the first step built nothing, confirmed the workspace was bare, and laid down the plan and the one rule that governs everything — every number is worked out once and shown the same way on every page, so a score can never disagree with itself. The second step turned that plan into a real, openable app — a permanent left-hand menu, the dark data-focused layout, and an honest status badge that tells you whether the data engine is connected and how fresh its data is — sitting on a foundation of about five and a half years of real daily price history for roughly 158 stocks and funds, frozen so the app runs entirely offline and answers the same way every restart, proven to span both a real downturn and a real rally.

The third step is where the first numbers reached the screen. The Sectors page came alive, ranking every sector and industry fund strongest-to-weakest with an A-to-E grade, its strength versus the broader market, its distance below its 12-month high, and a plain trend — each row expandable to show the reasons behind the grade. The Dashboard came alive too, reading the day's overall market mood as a labelled, scored figure with its drivers, how broadly that strength was spread, the data date, and the top sectors — drawn from the very same ranking the Sectors page uses. Where a number wasn't ready yet, it honestly showed "pending" instead of a made-up zero.

This latest step filled in the rest of the picture. The product now ranks every individual stock, giving each three plain grades — how strong it is, whether it's at a good buy point, and how risky it is — with a one-line reason, and lets you filter the list by sector or by setup type and click any name to see exactly what produced its grades. A new Themes page ranks investing themes like semiconductors and nuclear by price-confirmed strength. The Dashboard's two "pending" tiles now show real figures: how many stocks are ready to act on today, and the leading themes. Crucially, a stock's grades now read identically on every page. Next, each stock's own page gains a price chart with trend lines and a clear "wrong below this price" level.

What it can do today

The product lets users open a complete daily dashboard (market mood, breadth, top sectors, top themes, how many stocks are actionable, and the data date); browse and filter a full ranked list of individual stocks, each with three explainable grades and a reason; click any stock to see what drove its grades; rank investing themes by strength; rank every sector and industry; and rely on every score reading the same on every page. A per-stock price chart and a watchlist are still to come.
