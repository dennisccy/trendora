## Full tour (text only)

### Step 03 — Searching narrows the list, never the honesty

- **Narration:** Searching for a single company keeps every honesty label intact — nothing gets dressed up just because you're looking closer.
- **Action:** Type "AAPL" into "Search ticker or name…"
- **Point out:** The table narrows to matching rows as soon as you type.

### Step 04 — A stock pool that changes the way the real market does

- **Narration:** The universe of tracked companies isn't fixed in stone — names join and leave on their real, historical dates, and the Methodology page explains exactly how.
- **Action:** Navigate to /methodology
- **Point out:** The Universe Selection section describing how membership is decided.

### Step 09 — Blended signals face the same bar

- **Narration:** Combining multiple signals into one strategy doesn't earn it any shortcuts — every blend is held to the exact same honest standard.
- **Action:** Navigate to /research/factor-combination
- **Point out:** Each combination's own evidence status, right alongside the single-factor results.

### Step 10 — Every idea goes on record before it's tested

- **Narration:** Before any hypothesis is even tested, it's written down in a public registry — so nobody can quietly try many ideas and only report the lucky one.
- **Action:** Navigate to /research/registry
- **Point out:** The registry's list of every hypothesis, each with its own rationale.

### Step 11 — Failed ideas stay visible, never buried

- **Narration:** Ideas that didn't hold up aren't quietly deleted — they're kept in a browsable record, so nobody wastes time re-testing something already known not to work.
- **Action:** Navigate to /research/graveyard
- **Point out:** Rows marked permanently retired, right alongside their honest fail verdicts.

### Step 12 — A testing budget, spent in the open

- **Narration:** Every statistical test spends a little of a shared, limited budget — and that budget is shown openly before it's spent, never explained away after the fact.
- **Action:** Navigate to /research/budget
- **Point out:** How many tests have run so far, and how much budget is left.

### Step 13 — Checking the checker

- **Narration:** Even the testing process itself gets audited, run against scrambled, fake data to prove it doesn't rubber-stamp things that aren't actually true.
- **Action:** Navigate to /research/referee-audit
- **Point out:** A tripwire check that honestly flags a real problem instead of hiding it.

### Step 14 — Your own watchlist

- **Narration:** Beyond the main leaderboard, you can keep a personal watchlist of the names you care about most.
- **Action:** Navigate to /watchlist
- **Point out:** The watchlist page, ready for your own picks.
