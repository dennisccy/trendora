How it came together

The product started this session with a solid foundation — a working scanner, ranked leaderboards, stock detail pages, walk-forward backtest evidence, a persistent watchlist, a Factor Research Lab, and a Data Manager for imports. The first several iterations hardened the foundations: every date became a consistent YYYY-MM-DD format, historical date links started surviving a fresh tab or page reload, the dashboard gained a major-indexes chart with regime-band overlays, and the Methodology page became a searchable catalog of over 100 plain-language definitions with inline tooltips on every dense surface.

Subsequent waves added breadth. Column sorting, live ticker search, a theme filter, expandable theme member panels, a sortable samples drill-down, sector ETF names and member panels, and stage-aware import resume all arrived together. A trading-day availability heatmap, a calendar date popover, and an overlap-honest event study followed. Then five realized forward-return columns appeared on the stock leaderboard, the Themes and Sectors leaderboards, and every stock's detail page — plus five max-drawdown columns in a magnitude-graded colour scale, all reading from the same stored walk-forward calculation, never recomputed. The Regime x Setup x Pattern study arrived with NA-last sorting, filter dropdowns, and drill-down counts. The Stocks leaderboard header gained the selected date's regime label, score, and a ranked Top-5 themes strip.

The product was then extended with a Market Phase and Severity chapter. A discrete market-phase label and 0-100 severity score appeared on the dashboard, alongside a P(bear) probability from a deterministic Hamilton filter, a phase history timeline, dated causal downtrend episodes, a fenced retrospective sub-view, a Recovery-Turn Edge study, a Downtrend Opportunity study, and a FRED macro feed panel on the Data Manager.

The final chapter tackled the honest point-in-time universe. The scanner was rebuilt from scratch so the stocks leaderboard shows the true membership for every past date — empty before October 2021 and growing naturally to 544 stocks by today, not a fixed static list. A membership timeline chart and per-date coverage diagnostic appeared on the Data Manager page. This correct data exposed a performance problem: the Data Manager page began hanging because it re-computed over 1,300 dates of membership on every page load. Two further iterations resolved this: caching the membership timeline keyed to the dataset version, and then correcting a load-once bug where stocks with no price history were being re-read repeatedly during parallel jobs. When the full backend test suite flushed 977 passing and 0 failing, and a live browser session confirmed both the membership chart and the coverage diagnostic rendered correctly, the goal was declared achieved.
