## Highlights

### Step 01 — Open the daily dashboard  [NEW]

- **Narration:** Trendora opens on the daily dashboard — your one-glance read on the market. It pulls today's market regime, market breadth, and the strongest sectors into a single screen, so you can size up the day in seconds.
- **Action:** Navigate to /
- **Point out:** The 'Market Regime' card shows a colour-coded label (e.g. Risk-on) with a 0-100 score and the components behind it. Three breadth cards on the right are each marked 'universe-relative', a 'Data as-of' date sits top-right, and the Candidate Counts and Top Themes cards honestly read 'pending' instead of faking a zero.
- **Screenshot:** reports/demo/goal-i_can_see_the_wealthy_future-iter-2/step-01.png

### Step 02 — Open the full sector leaderboard  [NEW]

- **Narration:** Those Top Sectors are only the head of a full ranking. A click on 'Sectors' in the sidebar opens the complete leaderboard of every sector and industry ETF, ranked by its Sector Score.
- **Action:** Click the "Sectors" link
- **Point out:** A dense table ordered highest-score first — each row carries an A-E score badge, its strength versus the SPY benchmark, how far it trades below its 52-week high, and a trend label. SPY appears only as the excluded benchmark, never as a ranked leader.
- **Screenshot:** reports/demo/goal-i_can_see_the_wealthy_future-iter-2/step-02.png

### Step 03 — Expand a row to see what drove the score  [NEW]

- **Narration:** Click any row to unfold the reasons behind its score — a ranking should never be a number you have to take on trust.
- **Action:** Click "tbody tr:first-child"
- **Point out:** The top row expands into a Component / Detail / Contribution grid: relative strength over 1m, 3m and 6m, the moving-average stack, distance from the 52-week high, and volume trend — each with the contribution it added to the score.
- **Screenshot:** reports/demo/goal-i_can_see_the_wealthy_future-iter-2/step-03.png
