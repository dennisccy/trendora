# Backtest

Time-machine to a past scan date and read its forward-test scorecard — how that date's ranked cohort actually performed over the next 1/5/10/20/60 trading days vs SPY/QQQ/sector and a random same-sector control.

Survivorship bias

Walk-forward evidence now spans up to ~30 years of history (1996 to present, each name from its real first bar), but it is measured over a candidate pool built from CURRENT index members and therefore carries survivorship bias: names that were delisted or dropped along the way are absent from the whole span, so realized forward returns may be overstated. Read the edge as an upper bound, not a guarantee.
