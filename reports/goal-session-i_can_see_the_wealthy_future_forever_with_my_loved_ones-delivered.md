## What you can do today

- See today's market regime score and label — from strong risk-on to full risk-off — at a glance on the dashboard, alongside candidate counts, top-ranked sectors, top themes, market breadth, and a full-history chart of five major benchmarks with color-coded regime bands. The chart opens on the full available history by default.
- Browse a fully ranked stock leaderboard where every row shows three independent scores (Leadership, Entry Quality, Risk) as A–E grades with the number behind each, a setup status, a plain-language reason, and five realized forward-return columns (1-, 5-, 10-, 20-, and 60-day) showing how that stock actually performed after each historical scan date.
- Search by ticker or company name as you type; filter by sector, setup status, theme, or detected pattern (VCP and others); sort by any column including the five forward-return horizons — all filters and sort compose instantly with no page reload.
- Open any stock's detail page in a new tab to see a full price chart through the latest date with regime-band overlays, a per-bar hover box (date, OHLC, volume, percent change, and moving-average values), a concrete invalidation level, theme memberships, a named-component breakdown of all three scores, and a forward-return panel showing realized returns at five horizons for the selected date.
- Step back to any past trading day using a calendar popover that marks exactly which dates have saved snapshots — press the left or right arrow key to step one snapshot at a time. The correct historical data appears from the very first paint with no flicker. All pages and links follow the one global date; copy any URL or open a new tab and the same date is preserved.
- Browse the Themes leaderboard — themes ranked by score with 1-month and 3-month basket returns and breadth, with member lists that expand fully in place, each member linking to the dated stock detail in a new tab.
- Browse the Sectors leaderboard — every ETF row shows its config-defined name and description (never a bare ticker code) and expands to show the exact universe stocks mapped to that sector or industry group, each with a dated new-tab link.
- Open the Backtest workspace to read walk-forward forward-return evidence: by score bucket (A–E), by setup type, by market regime, and excess return vs SPY, QQQ, and the sector ETF — plus a control-group comparison against random same-sector peers — all scoped to snapshots available as of the selected date, with honest sample sizes and partial-horizon disclosures.
- Explore the Research Lab's Factor Lab: decile returns and Rank-IC per factor, multi-factor composite cohort combinations, and regime-conditioned effectiveness — all derived once from stored data, never recomputed per request. Each lab section loads independently so a slow computation does not freeze the page.
- Use the Setup and Pattern Lab event study in its overlap-honest default mode: each setup or pattern occurrence is counted once at the first scan date it triggered, not on every repeated consecutive day. A one-click "Pooled" toggle restores the prior per-signal-day figures for comparison, with a disclosure line always showing sample count, distinct symbols, and distinct episodes.
- Study the new Regime x Setup x Pattern ranked table in the Research Lab — a sortable breakdown showing which combinations of market regime, setup status, and detected chart pattern have historically produced the strongest risk-adjusted results, with drill-down links to the exact stored observations.
- Click any "N=" sample count anywhere in the Research Lab to open the exact stored observations in a new tab — sortable and filterable by ticker — with the row total always equal to the published count, and each row linking to the dated stock detail.
- Read over 120 plain-language definitions on the Methodology page — a searchable, categorized glossary covering every score, bucket, setup, pattern, regime label, and domain term — and see the same definitions as inline tooltips on every dense column header throughout the app.
- Save stocks to a persistent watchlist with the date you added them, your reason, current scores, setup status, price change since you added it, and an invalidation level.
- Manage price-data imports from the Data Manager: see a trading-day availability heatmap in a multi-hue color scale with a legend so coverage density is readable at a glance; read plain-language coverage explanations; pull exactly the missing data in one click; watch live per-symbol progress with a heartbeat timestamp; resume a rate-limited or interrupted job from the exact stage where it stopped with zero re-fetching; see every job appear in Run History the moment it starts; run multi-month or full-history backfill jobs reliably with a single failing date isolated while the rest complete; and remove imported data behind a confirmation dialog that shows a count summary — the committed seed is never deletable.

## How it came together

The product started this session with a solid foundation — a working scanner, ranked leaderboards, stock detail pages, walk-forward backtest evidence, a persistent watchlist, a Factor Research Lab, and a Data Manager for imports. The first four iterations closed the remaining surface gaps: every date throughout the app became a consistent YYYY-MM-DD format, historical date links started surviving a fresh tab or page reload, the dashboard gained a major-indexes chart with regime-band overlays, and the Methodology page became a searchable catalog of over 100 plain-language definitions with inline tooltips on every dense surface. The original goal was declared achieved.

With the owner's approval, the session continued in waves. Iterations 5 through 8 added column sorting and new-tab ticker links on the leaderboard, upgraded the dashboard indexes chart to show the full stored price history with a labelled date marker, completed the Research Lab evidence chain so every sample-count figure links to the exact stored observations, and made multi-date backfill run roughly four times faster in parallel with per-stage timings visible on each job card.

Iterations 9 through 11 added a live search box, a Themes column with a filter dropdown, expandable theme member lists with dated new-tab links, a click-sortable and ticker-filterable samples drill-down, and a transformed Sectors leaderboard where every industry ETF gained a config-defined name, description, and a member panel listing exactly which universe stocks belong to it.

Iterations 12 through 14 hardened the data pipeline and made research evidence more honest: stage-aware resume so interrupted jobs never re-fetch already-downloaded data, instant Run History entries the moment a job starts, per-date failure isolation so a single bad date never aborts a multi-date backfill, a trading-day availability heatmap on the Data Manager page, a calendar-style date popover, and an overlap-honest event study that collapses consecutive same-stock signal-days into one first-trigger observation by default — with a toggle to restore the original per-signal-day view.

Iterations 15 through 19 added the final layer of reliability and polish: reliable multi-month backfill, a deliberate range-scoped data removal flow with a confirmation summary, a multi-hue heatmap with a color-scale legend, a per-bar hover detail box on the stock chart, keyboard stepping for the date picker, synchronous URL hydration so historical date links never flash today's figures before switching, and the dashboard indexes chart defaulting to the full available history.

Iterations 20 and 21 delivered the final batch of research and evidence upgrades. Five realized forward-return columns (1-, 5-, 10-, 20-, and 60-day) now appear on both the stock leaderboard and each stock's own detail page — color-graded and honestly blank when not enough time has passed. A new Regime x Setup x Pattern study in the Research Lab ranks every observed combination of market regime, setup status, and chart pattern by historical risk-adjusted returns, with drill-down links to the exact observations. The Research page's sections now load independently so a slow computation no longer freezes the page. A final housekeeping iteration fixed two internal test-registry entries and turned the full 834-test automated suite completely green, formally confirming all three features. In total, 75 of the 78 Must-have journeys are passing; the remaining three are honestly blocked by an external data provider wall and require no code change to resolve once access is available.
