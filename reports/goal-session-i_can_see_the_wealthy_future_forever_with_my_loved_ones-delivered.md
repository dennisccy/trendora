## What you can do today

- See today's market regime score and label — from strong risk-on to full risk-off — at a glance on the dashboard, alongside candidate counts, top-ranked sectors, top themes, market breadth, and a full-history chart of five major benchmarks with colour-coded regime bands. The chart opens on the full available history by default.
- See the dashboard's Market Phase and Severity panel: a discrete market-phase label, a 0-100 severity number, a named component breakdown, and a P(bear) probability from a deterministic filter — all computed strictly from data available on or before the selected date, with no lookahead.
- Browse a phase history timeline on the dashboard showing every regime transition back to the earliest data, with dated causal downtrend episodes and a fenced retrospective sub-view so past calls can be audited without confusion.
- Browse a fully ranked stock leaderboard where every row shows three independent scores (Leadership, Entry Quality, Risk) as A–E grades, a setup status, a plain-language reason, an invalidation level, and five realized forward-return columns (1-, 5-, 10-, 20-, and 60-day) — plus five max-drawdown columns in a magnitude-graded colour scale so you can see both the gain and the worst dip you would have endured. The leaderboard correctly reflects the honest point-in-time universe for every past date: empty before October 2021 and growing naturally to roughly 544 stocks by today.
- See the selected date's market-regime label and score at the top of the Stocks leaderboard, plus a ranked strip showing the top five themes in order — the same stored values as the Dashboard, so the context for every stock is always visible.
- Search by ticker or company name as you type; filter by sector, setup status, theme, or detected pattern (VCP and others); sort by any column including the five forward-return horizons and the five max-drawdown horizons — all filters and sorts compose instantly with no page reload.
- Open any stock's detail page in a new tab to see a full price chart through the latest date with regime-band overlays, a per-bar hover box (date, OHLC, volume, percent change, and moving-average values), a concrete invalidation level, theme memberships, a named-component breakdown of all three scores, and a forward-return panel showing realized returns and max drawdowns at five horizons for the selected date.
- Step back to any past trading day using always-visible back and forward buttons in the top bar, optional left/right keyboard arrow keys, or a calendar popover that marks exactly which dates have saved snapshots — with year and month jump menus for quick navigation. The correct historical data appears from the very first paint with no flicker and no mismatch between what the server sent and what the browser shows. All pages follow the one global date; copy any URL or open it in a new tab and the same date is preserved.
- Browse the Themes leaderboard — themes ranked by score with member lists that expand fully in place, each member linking to the dated stock detail in a new tab, plus five forward-return columns and five max-drawdown columns on every theme row — the same figures the Backtest page shows, byte-identical.
- Browse the Sectors leaderboard — every ETF row shows its config-defined name and description, expands to show the universe stocks mapped to that sector or industry group with dated new-tab links, plus ten forward-return and max-drawdown columns matching Backtest exactly.
- Open the Backtest workspace to read walk-forward forward-return evidence: by score bucket, by setup type, by market regime, and excess return vs SPY, QQQ, and the sector ETF — plus a control-group comparison against random same-sector peers — all scoped to snapshots available as of the selected date, with honest sample sizes and partial-horizon disclosures.
- Explore the Research Lab's Factor Lab: decile returns and Rank-IC per factor, multi-factor composite cohort combinations, and regime-conditioned effectiveness — all loading independently so one slow study never freezes the page.
- Explore a Recovery-Turn Edge study (downtrend-exit edge) and a Downtrend Opportunity study on the Research page — both overlap-honest and pooled-default, with drill-down links to the exact stored observations.
- Use the Setup and Pattern Lab event study in its overlap-honest default mode — each occurrence is counted once at first trigger, not on every repeated consecutive day. A one-click "Pooled" toggle restores the per-signal-day view.
- Study the Regime x Setup x Pattern ranked table — a breakdown of which combinations of market regime, setup status, and detected chart pattern have historically produced the strongest results, with filter dropdowns, NA-last sorting, and drill-down links.
- Click any "N=" sample count anywhere in the Research Lab to open the exact stored observations in a new tab — sortable and filterable by ticker — with each row linking to the dated stock detail.
- Read over 120 plain-language definitions on the Methodology page — a searchable, categorized glossary covering every score, bucket, setup, pattern, regime label, and domain term — and see the same definitions as inline tooltips on every dense column header throughout the app.
- Save stocks to a persistent watchlist with the date you added them, your reason, current scores, setup status, price change since you added it, and an invalidation level.
- Manage price-data imports from the Data Manager: see a multi-hue availability heatmap with a colour-scale legend; read per-date coverage explanations; pull exactly the missing data; watch live per-symbol progress; resume a rate-limited or interrupted import from the exact stage where it stopped with zero re-fetching; see every job appear in Run History the moment it starts; run multi-month backfill jobs reliably; view a membership timeline chart showing how the honest point-in-time universe grew from October 2021 to today; inspect a per-date coverage diagnostic with admitted and excluded counts and reasons; trigger a confirm-gated full snapshot rebuild when the universe expands; and remove imported data behind a confirmation dialog that always protects the committed seed.
- View a FRED macro feed panel on the Data Manager page listing economic series and macro proxies that inform the regime model.

## How it came together

The product started this session with a solid foundation — a working scanner, ranked leaderboards, stock detail pages, walk-forward backtest evidence, a persistent watchlist, a Factor Research Lab, and a Data Manager for imports. The first several iterations hardened the foundations: every date became a consistent YYYY-MM-DD format, historical date links started surviving a fresh tab or page reload, the dashboard gained a major-indexes chart with regime-band overlays, and the Methodology page became a searchable catalog of over 100 plain-language definitions with inline tooltips on every dense surface.

Subsequent waves added breadth. Column sorting, live ticker search, a theme filter, expandable theme member panels, a sortable samples drill-down, sector ETF names and member panels, and stage-aware import resume all arrived together. A trading-day availability heatmap, a calendar date popover, and an overlap-honest event study followed. Then five realized forward-return columns appeared on the stock leaderboard, the Themes and Sectors leaderboards, and every stock's detail page — plus five max-drawdown columns in a magnitude-graded colour scale, all reading from the same stored walk-forward calculation, never recomputed. The Regime x Setup x Pattern study arrived with NA-last sorting, filter dropdowns, and drill-down counts. The Stocks leaderboard header gained the selected date's regime label, score, and a ranked Top-5 themes strip.

The product was then extended with a Market Phase and Severity chapter. A discrete market-phase label and 0-100 severity score appeared on the dashboard, alongside a P(bear) probability from a deterministic Hamilton filter, a phase history timeline, dated causal downtrend episodes, a fenced retrospective sub-view, a Recovery-Turn Edge study, a Downtrend Opportunity study, and a FRED macro feed panel on the Data Manager.

The final chapter tackled the honest point-in-time universe. The scanner was rebuilt from scratch so the stocks leaderboard shows the true membership for every past date — empty before October 2021 and growing naturally to 544 stocks by today, not a fixed static list. A membership timeline chart and per-date coverage diagnostic appeared on the Data Manager page. This correct data exposed a performance problem: the Data Manager page began hanging because it re-computed over 1,300 dates of membership on every page load. Two further iterations resolved this: caching the membership timeline keyed to the dataset version, and then correcting a load-once bug where stocks with no price history were being re-read repeatedly during parallel jobs. When the full backend test suite flushed 977 passing and 0 failing, and a live browser session confirmed both the membership chart and the coverage diagnostic rendered correctly, the goal was declared achieved.
