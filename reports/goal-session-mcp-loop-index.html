Project Goal

Trendora already produces explainable, regime-aware equity-leadership rankings (three independent scores — Leadership, Entry Quality, Risk — plus market regime/phase and realized forward-return evidence). The next evolution makes every signal **provable, not just explainable**: each score, ranking, and "edge" the user sees carries a visible **evidence status** sourced from an append-only **evidence ledger** of out-of-sample, control-beating, multiple-testing-corrected claims. Unvalidated signals are shown as *"not yet proven"* — never as confident numbers. The platform improves itself by mining its own data for candidate decision-support views and shipping only the ones a statistical **referee** certifies. This is research-only decision support; it never predicts returns or places orders.

The story so far

Project story so far

Trendora is a market-leadership ranking tool that shows traders which scoring signals have genuinely earned their confidence labels — through rigorous out-of-sample statistical testing, not in-sample curve-fitting.

How it has grown

Trendora began this session with its analytical machinery already in place — Leadership, Entry Quality, and Risk scores; a market-regime tracker; sector leaderboards; a research lab — but with no user-facing evidence surface. The first two iterations built that surface: "Proven" and "Not yet proven" chips appeared on every score, a dedicated Evidence ledger page went live, and Leadership became the first signal to earn a certified label (+6.36% vs. SPY). Iterations 3 through 8 deepened the evidence layer: a Breakout-watch regime edge was certified (+6.12% in strong-market conditions), and the volatility-contraction pattern earned its first "Proven" badge in the Research Factor Lab at the 20-day horizon (+3.33%).

Iterations 9 and 10 introduced a disciplined private practice ledger running under stricter multiple-testing rules — a safe space to test ideas without inflating the canonical bar. The first sweep found that the volatility-contraction pattern also held at the 60-day horizon (+8.91% holdout edge). Iteration 11 promoted that winner to the canonical ledger and evolved the Factor Lab into a per-horizon strip, so users can now see certified edges at both the 20-day and 60-day windows.

Iteration 12 repeated the same two-step approach for two-factor combinations. Three pre-approved pairs ran through the strict out-of-sample referee; two failed honestly. The third — momentum leaders (relative strength vs. SPY) that are also near their 52-week high — passed comfortably, clearing even the stricter Bonferroni bar with comfortable margin. The win was recorded privately, ready to display.

Iteration 13 built out that display. The Multi-factor combination lab's composite cohort row gained an honest evidence badge that reads "Proven" only for the one certified pair and "Not yet proven" for everything else. A new 6th row appeared on the Evidence page with the full backing details — both factor conditions, the out-of-sample verdict, a +4.69% holdout edge beating SPY, and a link back to the combination lab. Twelve of fourteen browser checks passed. A minor anchor-scroll fix was identified and applied during the iteration; a final browser check in iteration 14 will confirm the fix and allow all required journeys to be declared complete.

What it can do today

The product lets users browse the stock leaderboard and see "Proven" or "Not yet proven" on every score; expand the proof panel on any Leadership card to read the sealed out-of-sample evidence (+6.36% vs. SPY); confirm Entry Quality and Risk are honestly labeled "not yet proven"; follow the Breakout-watch setup's certified edge in strong-market conditions; browse the Evidence page with six certified claims and round-trip links to each research surface; see the volatility-contraction pattern marked "Proven" in the Research Factor Lab at both the 20-day and 60-day horizons. The combination lab's "Proven" badge for the certified momentum + proximity-to-high pairing is functionally complete and awaiting final browser confirmation in iteration 14.
