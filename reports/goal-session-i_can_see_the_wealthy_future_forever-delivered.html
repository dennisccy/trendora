Delivered — Trendora: a local-first, research-only US equity leadership scanner

Session: icanseethewealthyfutureforever Date: 2026-06-04 Final verdict: GOAL_ACHIEVED Iterations: 20 (a starting-point check-up plus 19 build rounds)

What you can do today

- See the market at a glance. Open the dashboard for the day's market mood, the strongest sectors and themes, how many stocks look actionable, the market breadth, and when the last scan ran. - Browse and filter ranked leaderboards. Explore ranked stocks, sectors, and themes; narrow the stock list to a single sector, a trade setup, or any of three chart patterns — and bookmark or share the exact filtered view as a link. - Open any stock's full scorecard. Read three independent, plain-English scores — how strong the stock is, whether the price is a good place to enter, and how risky it is — each broken into the parts that produced it, alongside the price level that would prove the idea wrong. The numbers read the same on the list and on the detail page. - Rewind the whole app to any past day. One shared date control moves the entire product — dashboard, leaderboards, detail pages, and evidence — to a chosen past trading day, showing only what was known then. A stock's chart still keeps drawing right up to today, with the chosen day clearly marked, so you can see what actually happened next. - Read forward-tested evidence. On the Backtest page, see how the higher-ranked picks actually performed afterwards — by score grade, against the market and sector benchmarks, and against a fair random same-sector comparison — as of any past date, and break any result down into the individual stocks, sectors, and ranking tiers that drove it. - Dig into the Research area. Test whether any signal really sorted future returns — sorted into ten tiers with a single correlation score, shown raw and on a downside-risk-aware basis, split by calm-versus-turbulent markets, blended across many signals at once, and across a whole family of volatility measures — and study any setup or chart pattern's full track record: its typical gain, how often it finished positive, the worst dip and best rise along the way, and the best holding length. Read every figure either across all history or rewound to a chosen past date. - Travel from evidence to action. Jump straight from a research finding to the live names expressing it today, and on to any one stock's full scorecard. - Keep your own watchlist. Save stocks with a reason; the list is still there after a full restart. - Grow the dataset yourself. Add more days of history by a single date or a whole range and watch it backfill live, ending in a clear success-or-failure summary — and never a made-up price. - Look everything up. A plain-language glossary explains every score, label, trade setup, and chart pattern.

Throughout, the product is built to earn trust rather than push a trade: it ranks, explains, shows what would prove an idea wrong, and — wherever there isn't enough history to be sure — honestly says "not enough data yet" instead of inventing a number. On defensive, risk-off days it holds back every "actionable" label.

How it came together

The starting point. Trendora arrived already substantially built — a dense, dark analytical dashboard that ranks US stocks, sectors, and themes after the market closes, with every score explained and every idea showing the price that would prove it wrong. The first round was a careful check-up to record exactly what worked and what was still missing.

Closing the original gaps. Early rounds tightened the product into a coherent whole: one shared date control that moves every screen together (the backtest screen lost its separate calendar), a way to open any forward-tested return into the individual stocks, sectors, and ranking tiers behind it, and a new Data Manager screen to grow the dataset on demand without ever inventing prices. A closure pass then confirmed the rest end to end — filters that narrow the list honestly, the volatility-squeeze pattern tools, identical scores across pages, a watchlist that survives a restart, and fast page loads — completing every one of the nineteen original must-have goals.

A bigger ambition. The owner then set a larger target: a much wider universe of stocks, shorter chart timeframes, and a set of research labs. Charts learned to keep drawing past a chosen past date all the way to today (clearly marked, and never changing the scores), and the backtest page began showing the real return each top sector, theme, and stock went on to deliver at a chosen holding length.

A data wall, handled honestly. Widening the watched list from about 120 names toward roughly 500 needed a one-time download of real price history, and the free data source kept refusing requests. Rather than fabricate any numbers to force the bigger list through, the team left the universe exactly as it was, kept the new "how the universe is selected" panel hidden until a genuine screen could run, and turned to the data already in hand.

The Research area opens. Working only with data it already had, the scanner learned to spot two more chart patterns beyond the original one, then a whole Research area opened. Its Factor Lab lets you test whether a signal actually sorted future returns — by tier, by market mood, blended across several signals at once, and across a family of volatility measures. A Setup & Pattern Lab studies how every past occurrence of a setup or pattern really played out, and a synthesis step lets you travel from any finding straight to the names behind it and on to a stock's scorecard.

The re-scope and the polish. The owner returned and re-scoped the goal so the data-blocked items would no longer hold up the project. The historical track record was consolidated onto a single, rewindable Backtest page (the old separate "System Health" page was retired so everything now sits under the one shared date control), and the multi-signal combination was made genuinely useful — blending the chosen signals into one reliably-filled ranking instead of the old "meets every signal at once" line that was almost always empty.

The finishing touch. The last build gave the Research area its own "All history ⟷ As of date" switch, so every research figure can be read either across all history or rewound to a chosen past day — seeing only what was known by then, and honestly marking "not enough data yet" at early dates. Crucially, this stayed a mode, not a second calendar: the whole app still has exactly one shared date control. With that switch in place, every buildable goal was met and the product was delivered. The only outstanding wishes — a much larger stock list and intraday chart timeframes — remain honestly recorded as waiting on a data source that currently can't be reached, ready to fill in the moment a feed becomes available.
