What changed this time

The Research Lab's evidence drill-down table is now sortable and filterable. Click any column header on the samples page to re-order the rows (click again to reverse; a third click returns to the original order). When nothing matches, you get an honest message rather than empty or invented rows. Clearing the filter restores the full list. Also new this round: clicking an "N=" chip on the Research page now opens the drill-down in a new tab, so your Research lab selections and scroll position are never disturbed.
