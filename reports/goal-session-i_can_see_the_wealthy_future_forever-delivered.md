# Delivered — Trendora: a research-only US equity leadership scanner

**Session:** i_can_see_the_wealthy_future_forever
**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Final verdict:** GOAL_ACHIEVED
**Iterations:** 6 (a baseline review plus five build-and-verify rounds)

## What you can do today

- **See the market at a glance.** Open the dashboard for the day's overall market mood, the strongest sectors and themes, how many stocks look actionable, how broad the strength is, and when the last scan ran.
- **Browse and filter ranked lists.** Explore ranked leaderboards of stocks, themes, and sectors, and narrow the stock list down to a single sector, a setup type, or just the stocks showing the "VCP" volatility-squeeze chart pattern.
- **Open any stock for a plain-English scorecard.** See its price chart, three independent scores — how strong the stock is, whether the current price is a good place to enter, and how risky it is — each broken down into the reasons behind it, alongside the price level that would prove the idea wrong. The same scores read identically on the list and on the stock's own page.
- **Trust the evidence, not the hype.** Read forward-tested results showing how the higher-ranked picks actually performed against the market and against a fair random same-sector benchmark — then open any result up into the individual stocks, sectors, and ranking tiers that drove it, with its spread and hit-rate, so a weak number can be diagnosed rather than taken on faith.
- **Travel back in time.** Move the whole product to any past trading day with one shared date control and see exactly what the scanner said that day — past scans are never rewritten — or open the backtest screen for a single day's full scorecard.
- **Save and revisit your ideas.** Build a watchlist with your own notes that survives a full restart of the app, each entry showing the date added, its current scores and setup, the price since you added it, and a level that would invalidate the idea.
- **Grow the dataset yourself.** Use the Data Manager to add more market history by a single date or a whole range, watch a live progress bar fill the new days in, and read a clear success-or-failure summary; the new days immediately become available everywhere in the app.
- **Look everything up — honestly.** A plain-language glossary explains every score, label, and chart pattern. Throughout, defensive risk-off days hold back "actionable" picks, and anywhere there isn't enough data yet the product says so plainly instead of inventing numbers.

## How it came together

It started fully formed but unproven. Trendora arrived as a dense, dark analytical dashboard that already ranked the market after the close — stocks, themes, and sectors — and explained every score instead of just shouting "buy." The first round added nothing on purpose: a careful walk through every planned feature to record exactly what worked and what was still missing. It surfaced three gaps — no way to grow the dataset, a backtest screen with its own stray date menu, and no breakdown of what actually drove the results.

Next, the date controls were unified. The backtest screen's separate date menu was retired so that the single control at the top of the app now drives every screen. Pick a date once and it holds as you move around, the backtest screen included.

Then results became something you could open up. Instead of just a single average return, you can now see the individual stocks that drove a result up or dragged it down, how it splits across sectors and across ranking tiers, and its overall spread and hit-rate — turning a weak number into something you can actually explain.

After that came the Data Manager. This new screen lets you add more market history by a single date or a whole range: press Start, watch a live progress bar fill in the new days, and read a clear final summary. The new days immediately appear in the shared date control and the track record grows to cover them — and if a live price fetch can't reach its source, it says so plainly and never invents prices.

With the big pieces in place, the focus shifted to confirming everything held together. One round verified the stock-list filters end-to-end — narrowing to a single sector or setup, with an honest "nothing matches" message when a filter comes up empty — and confirmed the volatility-squeeze chart-pattern tools correctly flag, explain, and forward-test those setups.

The final round confirmed the last three things: that a stock's scores read identically on the list and on its own detail page, that a saved watchlist is still there after the app is fully restarted, and that the main screens load quickly from saved data. With those confirmed, every one of the nineteen planned capabilities works end-to-end, and the goal was declared complete.
