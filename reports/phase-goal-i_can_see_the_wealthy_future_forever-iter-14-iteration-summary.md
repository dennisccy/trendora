## In plain words

**What you can do now:** See the day's market at a glance; browse ranked stocks, sectors, and themes and filter the stock list by sector, setup type, or any of three chart patterns; open any stock for a plain-English scorecard — identical on the list and the detail page — plus the price that would prove the idea wrong; rewind the whole app to any past day with one shared date control and watch a price chart keep drawing past that day; read honest evidence of how past picks actually performed by stock, sector, and ranking tier; explore a Research area that tests whether a signal really sorted future returns — by group, by market mood, in combinations, and across a family of volatility measures — and now study how every past occurrence of a setup or chart pattern actually played out; save a watchlist that survives a restart; grow the dataset by date; and look up every label in a plain-language glossary — always with honest "not enough data yet" notes instead of made-up numbers.

**What changed this time:** You can now study a setup or chart pattern's full track record in one place. Pick something like "breakout watch" or "pullback to a rising average" and see how that kind of setup has actually performed across every past day the system has data for — its typical gain, how often it finished positive, the worst dip and best rise along the way, the best holding length, and how it differed by market mood and by sector — with honest "not enough history" marks (the rarely-seen default shows a count of two rather than inventing numbers).

**What's next:** Next the product will tie it all together — letting you start from a piece of lab evidence and travel straight to the live names behind it, all the way to a single stock's detail page.
