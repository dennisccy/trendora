This iteration is **Stage B1-completion** — it performs the migration and the fail-closed fix, but it does **not** begin Stage C (the destructive derived-state clear) or anything after it. J-10 is separately CLOSED by the owner (585/587 restored, EA/EQR named unrestorable) and needs no further action.

**Lessons applied directly:**
